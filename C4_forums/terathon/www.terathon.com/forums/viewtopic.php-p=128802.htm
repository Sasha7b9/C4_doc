Kickstarter Launched for The 31st

by Eric Lengyel » 06 Apr 2015, 15:34

Today, we launched a Kickstarter campaign for our game The 31st. This project represents an important and exciting opportunity for Terathon Software to complete a game that showcases the full potential of the C4 Engine. If the campaign is successfully funded, we'll be finishing The 31st for Halloween of this year, and it will stimulate a huge advancement in the features of the engine. Please help out as much as you can, preferably by making a contribution on the following page. There are plenty of interesting rewards for your support.

https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/876473683/the-31st

If you're flat broke, you can still help by spreading the word as widely as possible. Every licensed user of the C4 Engine has a lot to gain from the success of this project. Thanks in advance!
