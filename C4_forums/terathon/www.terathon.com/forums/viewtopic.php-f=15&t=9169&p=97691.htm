Voxelogic's Acropora - Voxel Terrain Generation

by Bumstead » 15 May 2010, 12:16

I really like the voxel terrain capabilities of the C4 Engine and I've been looking for a terrain generation tool that will generate terrains that take advantage of those capabilities. Most terrain generators are heightmap based so they don't create tunnels and overhangs automatically. And if they do, like Terragen 2, it's not possible to get non-heightmap data exported out of them unless it's as a 3D mesh.

I recently found a tool called Acropora by Voxelogic (http://www.voxelogic.com/). The samples they show aren't mindblowing but the idea of actually generating a 3D voxel field is really promising. And the best part is that they support exporting the generated voxel data (not a heightmap) as a raw file. The only missing piece is an import routine for C4 that takes this kind of file.

Just thought I'd let you all know about it.

Re: Voxelogic's Acropora - Voxel Terrain Generation

by Eric Lengyel » 21 May 2010, 13:51

It's just plain single-channel floating-point data with no header. For example, if you have a voxel map with 128x128x128 voxels, then a RAW file would be composed of exactly 8 MB of floating-point values, one per voxel. Acropora stores them in the order for which z changes most quickly, then y, and finally x. Y is up in Acropora, but the C4 importer lets you choose whether Y or Z is up.

Other things I discovered (and was told previously). The engine Creates a skin when the Voxel goes through the 0.0 position. Ie, the skin lies between 2 Voxels.
