by Eric Lengyel » 21 Sep 2006, 19:48

dakz0rz wrote:and working with ATI hardware too?


Yes, the new algorithm will work on all hardware supported by the engine. The previous (current) algorithm needs to access the stencil buffer as a texture, and this is only possible with Nvidia's NV_copy_depth_to_color extension. The new algorithm is going to sacrifice full penumbrae in favor of outer half-wedges only. This is much faster (at the cost of correctness) and doesn't require the coverage counts in the stencil buffer.
