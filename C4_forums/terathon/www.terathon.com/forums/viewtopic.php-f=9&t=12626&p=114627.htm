by cbken » 08 Feb 2013, 05:06

When you resize the window, you resize the OpenGL viewport to match the new size. How and what you then draw into the viewport is up to you. You can scale everything up or you can draw UI elements at a set pixel size. There's nothing particularly more slow about that than any other rendered frame. I mean, apps using OpenGL have windows resized all the time without any problems. I just don't get the claim that it would be too slow.
