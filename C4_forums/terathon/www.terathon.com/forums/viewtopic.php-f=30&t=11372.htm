Re: Burlap Doll Game?

by CodeNewt » 26 Nov 2011, 18:57

Well what I envision for this game is that the world is open for anyone to develop, I'd like for any user to be able to download the client and start making maps that they can share with friends or online. I plan on using the browser plugin to enable this ability to browse and download contributor's maps.

The premise of the game is primarily exploration with a mix of puzzling, adventure, and mild combat. Basically, I'm kind of building this game around the concepts that I personally enjoy out of RPG's and that is the exploration of the environment and getting to know the world around you. Less about killing endless hoards of monsters and grinding out the next level just to 'beat' the game. The game should be tailored to provide story and let the gamer get sucked into a new world.

Each map can have it's own storyline about the main character. Which, I'm thinking, will let this game lend itself to be called something along the lines of "The adventures of <blah>", where each map is a "Book" of a story for the main character. I will be developing maps and some stories for the main character myself, but I really hope others will enjoy this concept and make their own contributions!

Lastly, I just don't want the story to end with my own contributions. I want to see what other's can come up with.

Arthain-Baka wrote:Just my opinion, but I think the lighting from the screenshot with the caption "A room... for some kind of ritual?" seems to overpower the texture, it just seems to look too smooth especially compared the the others. But that could just be me...

Its that white'ish wall texture you are referring to, right? Yeah. I hate it, that texture is the first to be replaced when I get into another bout of texture drawing. My method for developing has gone in cycles, Models > Textures > Programming > World design back to Models > etc, its like whatever I'm in the mood to work on and I'll sit there for a couple of hours making new stuff or tweaking old stuff.

I have, absolutely, no idea how to get the bump mapping and parallax mapping to really pop out from this camera angle. Those options are only weighing down FPS without bringing much detail to the world. So I might disable them entirely.
Though that camera DOES zoom in automatically when the ceiling is lower (as to allow multiple levels in a cavern, without the camera going up one level and blinding you) which at that point the player would see those details much better.

So if anyone has some good advice on how to generate parallax for static objects and bump maps for textures that will really pop out, I'd really appreciate it!

Arthain-Baka wrote:And the screenshot with the caption "I feel like someone is watching me...", I think it looks pretty darn good for developer art.

Thanks! I've been working pretty hard at this for a while, so I really do appreciate that sentiment.

Also a note about the lighting; I have a script method that changes the ambient light setting (fading it to the new value and not just instantly flashing over in one frame) for the current zone the player is in.
It has helped pull out the mood I wanted in some rooms, because having independent light sources from the "torches" alone didn't brighten the room enough without creating some really sharp shadows.
