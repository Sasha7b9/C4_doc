Right, it's a clever conjoining of C4's realtime 'static' functionality with the real sophistication of a raytracer. It's been fun watching your methods evolve alongside your project.

I think you're going to have to solve the reverse behavior that accompanies shadows too, illumination. If all your assets are calibrated for your offline illumination, then any light influence will result in overexposure. There might be a clever use of object flag manipulation to get the result you want. Curious to see what you come up with.
