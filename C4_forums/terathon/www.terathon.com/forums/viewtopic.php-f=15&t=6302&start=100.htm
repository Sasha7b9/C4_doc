Re: Blender To C4

by CodeNewt » 20 Jun 2012, 10:18

mschifter wrote:OK, I think I see the problem now. Could you tell me where in the script it picks up the starting and ending frame? I think if I just edit your python script to hard code the selected animation frames that will enable me to pick out what I need into separate c4 animation files.

OR, you can make your animations separate in Blender and when you export 'selected animation' it will export your currently 'selected animation'.
This means not having all your animations in one animation loop. (AKA: not 1500-2000 frames for one animation, but 10 animations with 40-200 frames each)

When you make the separate animation loop, you can use ctrl+x to cut and paste keyframes from one animation to another.

Edit #2: I realize that the separate animations isn't something everyone knows how to do in blender, when you open up the animation editor by switching from default to animation view. At the bottom of the dope sheet editor you have a drop down next to the "key" button and "summary" field. This drop down should be initially on the "dope sheet" option, switch it over to "action editor". Now 2 fields to your right from this drop down is going to be the named field for each animation. Hit the plus sign to make a new 'action' and it will load up with the dope sheet again (but the bottom part will still be in action mode), from here you can make as many "actions" for your model as you want. Each separate action can be exported as a separate animation without any issue. (searching google for answers on how to make separate actions in blender might be useful, as there are videos on this)
