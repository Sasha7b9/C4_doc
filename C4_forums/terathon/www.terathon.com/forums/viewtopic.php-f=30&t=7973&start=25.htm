Just finished several rounds of the game. First off, let me congratulate you and your team on getting this far. It's all come together very nicely.

But, being that it's a beta, I must make some suggestions:

1. Fix the music
2. Replace the "hit" sound effect
3. Some collision issues around corners.
4. What's the point of jumping if you can fly? I remapped the fly button to the W key, and did away with the jump key entirely.
5. My biggest gripe - and what could potentially kill your chances in IGF/IGC - is the rocket launcher. It's way too powerful. I understand it's a rocket launcher and all, but still... several times I respawned and was promptly killed again by one rocket. As much as I hate to say it (I like uber weapons... so long as they're in my hands) the rocket launcher needs to be nerfed.

In spite of the above, it's still a great game. I particularly enjoyed the bot customization feature - something that most games lack. I created a "perfect" bot, with all stats set to max, and I'm pleased to say I held my own against him. Too bad I lost by one point.

As for future physics... stuff, might I suggest a pendulum-style rotary saw - a modern take on"The Pit and the Pendulum". Moving platforms, especially - make whatever is holding them up breakable.

Finally - a weapon suggestion: sticky mines.

by miked » 09 Oct 2009, 06:52

Ludi wrote:
miked wrote:Also, another idea - it would be cool to see the players' legs/thrusters adjust position based on the direction they are actually moving rather than being fixed. I can't really think of a way you would do this with the existing thruster design though.
How exactly should this work? Could you elaborate that a little bit more?


The idea is just to make it look more physically accurate. Think of launching a rocket - if the rocket is moving straight up, the thrusters are shooting straight down. Basically, having the thrusters shooting flames in the opposite direction that the character is moving (when the thrusters are active). Like I said, with how they are positioned on the character though I'm not really sure how you'd do this.

Edit: So for example, to move straight up, thrusters on the soles of their feet would activate, and then to move left or right, a thruster on the side of their leg would activate as well.
