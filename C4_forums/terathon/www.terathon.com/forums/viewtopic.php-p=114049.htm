This might be out of line since I dont really know what the C4 engine does internally and maybe you're laughing at me for suggesting something so trivial, but here it goes anyway. Are you using the hardware occlusion testing that DX and probably Open GL also provides? What I did at some point where I had clusters of objects, was to go through the list of objects, make one object per frame render last and then use that hardware interface in DX that tells you how many pixels were actually drawn on the screen. When it was under a certain threshold, I marked the object as low priority and didnt render it for a while until a new test was due. Of course opens the door for the possibility that an object pops into view too late when it becomes visible due to camera or other movements, but it enabled me to not render a bunch of objects that were occluded and the delay was usually not noticeable. Maybe this could be made optional or so.

Again, I apologize if this is too trivial or you are already using it, but I wanted to throw it in here for consideration.
