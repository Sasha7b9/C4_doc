Glad to hear it works in AU.

They have the option of swapping out the song with another "sponsored" one but from what i've seen youtubes library is extremely limited. I looked for an hour and didn't see anything that matched as good so if we want to use something else of our choosing we might have to re-upload or something else it looks like to allow everyone in the world to view it.

The last stress-test that was done by the networking aspect was in Austin in september. The test created 2000 simulated boxes (players on PCs) within a 1sqkm region with an average ping below 100. Once again though this was a local test without people from across the world trying to log in which could be an obstacle, but at least we have an idea of it's capabilities. We're hoping to cram on as many as 5K users simultaneous on our primary server.

That stuff was completely stripped out when we began our rewrite recently to rebuild the game with a unified coding convention (this was a concern when employing people from all around the world to work in synchronous) but from what I can see with my own two eyes things appear to work much smoother now and i'm hoping for another stress test in March, this time with more physical players.

Like I told another fellow who asked me about C4s networking and how we were pulling this off, in PMs, C4 isn't an MMO engine so it can't be expected to be able to pull off mmo-sized amounts of players, but there are other options out there if you can get some money together that plug into C4 quite well. What we use is called "netdog" and it's been very good to us and they were very receptive in negotiating an up front cost/royalties agreement for us that suited our needs so i would defiantly recommend them if you're sticking with C4.

You might want to look at Project Darkstar ( http://www.projectdarkstar.com ) which is a opensource MMO networking solution from SUN (I guess they're trying to sell hardware from it - although its not hardware specific). Probably for smaller games ( as it sounds like you are asking about ) it should be good to go.
