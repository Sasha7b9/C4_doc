Re: Joystick problems

by mohrens » 18 Oct 2008, 09:32

Erik, I could fix it,

The problem was that the InputControl for the LinearAxis case was not activated,
changing C4Input.cpp
within the
#if C4MACOS
void InputControl::Activate(IOHIDQueueInterface **deviceQueue)

the line
if ((controlType != kInputGroup) && (!(controlFlags & kInputPolled)))
(I changed it to just "true" which is not the right thing I am sure )
made it work

Best, Martin

Re: Joystick problems

by Eric Lengyel » 18 Oct 2008, 16:39

Thanks for looking into this. It turns out that the kInputPolled flag can just be removed from the engine altogether. So you can just change the line

Code: Select all
if ((controlType != kInputGroup) && (!(controlFlags & kInputPolled)))


to

Code: Select all
if (controlType != kInputGroup)


and everything will be fine.

Please do not post on topics that are over a year old. (In this case, the topic is over 6 years old.) Start a new topic instead.

To answer your question, the demo game supports joysticks on the Mac. After clicking on an item in the Control Settings dialog, you can push a button on the joystick to assign a control. For the "Horizontal look" and "Vertical look" items, you can move a joystick axis to assign it to those motions.
