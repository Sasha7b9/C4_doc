As you said you are a beginner I recommend you not to worry about these things at this stage. It is far better to build up your programming and C++ skills as a priority.

I know it's very interesting to know, but I too fall into these traps when I was new into games. Thinking and worrying too much spending all the time researching but got nothing done.

The truth is that you won't know until you test it yourself, it depends many things such as how you programmed it and how efficient the art assets are. Whether they are skinned, how many materials, AI, other static objects....list goes on...

For a general guideline you can check out the C4 Demo (If you haven't yet) using you own hardware.

Definitely start out simple for your first project
