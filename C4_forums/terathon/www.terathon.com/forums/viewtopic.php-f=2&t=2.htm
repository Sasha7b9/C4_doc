by Jesse Meyer » 15 Jun 2010, 12:40

Now we're getting somewhere, I think.

Before we were talking about game engines, but it seems you're more referring to games themselves. You clearly stated that artists are the ones to thank for making things 'pretty'. This is true. You have the programmers to thank for empowering them with the tools to get around any limitations they need to overcome.

Not everything in TGE looks like crap, but I've seen plenty of things in C4 look like crap. I've seen art in CryEngine(2) look terrible. I've also seen beautiful work in all these engines.

You cannot really compare different 'gen' engines effectively. C4 probably supported the same graphical effects (I'm not sure, but if not, it only took a year to 'catch-up') Doom3 did. They are then, the same gen engine and it's worthwhile to compare. GoldSRC vs Source are entirely different gens, so comparisons (in this case) are misleading -- but your points are still true, nonetheless.

There is no blanket answer for your question. It depends on the game and the target PCs really.
