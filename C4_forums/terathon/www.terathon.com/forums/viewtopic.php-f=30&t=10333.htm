C4 Kinect - Update [Released] (14/3/11)

by Arthain-Baka » 13 Feb 2011, 03:49

Update: You can now find this inside the Community Tools, or search for "C4Kinect Plugin".

Some of you (Eric Lengyel and JamesH) have probably noticed my annoying posts about surrounding a mysterious "Device", first off, a big thanks to those two who helped me with my silly questions (and my sympathy to JamesH for being too slow (He knows what I mean) ).

As I'm not one for words I'm just going to post the pictures (Sorry there isn't many atm) so behold the beginnings of the C4 Kinect Project!

Prototype_001.jpg
Prototype_002.jpg


Don't worry about the bad hand display on the depth image, that's because I was too close to the sensor when I took the screenshot and hadn't configured the depth Cutoff points correctly.

Update (22/2/11)
You guys may or may not have noticed the delay from my expected release, unfortunately a number of things have been going wrong recently including my NAS (which amoung other high priorities stores my CVS repositories) took a turn for the worth the other day and kept dropping the RAID array and other issues so I was forced to do some backups and find out the issue, which I'm still working on.

However, the Plugin is functioning with most of the functionality at a base level, and exposes a few user callbacks, device creation/destruction and a little management, as well as exposing the required functions to access a Users skeleton information. As I don't know how much time I'll have to work on it for a little while I figured I'd let people know and give them a choice, either I can hold off on releasing it and release it later (which may be a week or two at least) or I can release what there currently is to the community, so just let me know what you think in replies.

Yes the plugin and all code related will be released, I've just got to stop watching movies and finish it .
It'll be more of a point to develop from though.

A little spoiler as to what I've added recently is that the plugin handle states and stuff and will let you set callbacks for events like "NewUser" or "UserLost" for application dependent events (Like say pausing on user lost) which will be called after internal processing during said events.

In progress at the moment is a controller that can be used to control a rigged skeleton, worst part is I don't have a nice model to do this with just a little box based one I made .
