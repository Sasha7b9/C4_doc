Re: Slow Licensced Download

by Szymon Połom » 06 Jan 2009, 04:18

I got normal pings to http://www.terathon.com from various connections (~170 ms which is okay for an oversee connection).

Trace routing is an indication, but not really meaningful, some routers don't care about responding to pings (they prefer to route the real traffic instead ) and some routers have a high priority on responding to pings. I think it's more of a traffic shaping problem than latency/bottleneck issues, I assume it's done on purpose by some carrier and/or its peers.

That being said, I only know some of the "bad" carriers & peers in Europe.
