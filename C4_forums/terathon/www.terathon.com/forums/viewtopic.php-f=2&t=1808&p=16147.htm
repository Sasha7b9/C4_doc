Frank,

From what I've read, on windows at least (should be same for mac) full-screen mode should always be quite a bit faster than windowed mode. This is why all the major games tend to run full-screen. The reason has to do with some effeciencies (i.e. "flipping") that the gpu can do with its buffers when it doesn't have to worry about other windows.

by jwatte » 18 Feb 2007, 00:05

Btw, Windows Vista does NOT ship with OpenGL drivers for anything but Microsoft compatibility mode (which implements version 1.4, and is pretty slow). You MUST get OpenGL drivers for Vista from your graphics vendor (ATI or NVIDIA) to run accelerated OpenGL with all features that C4 needs. Such drivers may or may not already be available for download, depending on vendor, Vista version, etc.
