Re: Wind of Luck - a pirate MMO game

by ca$per » 21 Feb 2014, 03:12

1. Number of models (islands) depends on the location's characteristics. Each island should have somewhere about ~5k triangles. The texturing depends on the artist. We do have different types of texturing techniques used: splating, single texture for entire location, detail textures, etc. We were planing to use 4 types of textures: ambient, diffuse, specular, normal. But that's not mandatory. If an artist desides to use just diffuse+normal (for example) to make them look good, then it's ok. All geometry is static. No dynamic or animated meshes. No skinning or anything like that. Just static islands. We do have an ocean shader already, but it should be tuned for each location to accomodate for color balance.

2. We need an artist to prepare a final .wld file for us. That means all materials, node names and node hierarchy. There are no scripts or animations, at least we don't see where they should be used.

Also each location would need a proper skybox created.

Re: Wind of Luck - a pirate MMO game

by Trazzy » 06 Jun 2014, 07:57

Wind of Luck: Arena update – New features!

Ladies and gentlemen of fortune on the high seas!

We are happy to announce that a new Wind of Luck: Arena update is now live with full-scale game features including shipyard,
ship progression, store, arsenal, achievements, as well as many others.
Come join fierce sea battles in different battle locations including East Asia, the Caribbean and even the Arctic.

User Interface Changes

- Numerous enhancements have been made in graphic settings and fonts.
- Many GUI elements were redone to achieve the best readability possible.
- The ribbon and an ensign for tracking wind direction were added.

Gameplay Changes

- Two new locations “Imperial Pearls” Asia and "Lost Expedition" Arctic have been added.
- Based on battle results the player will receive piastres, fame points and skill points.
- You may now explore and buy new ships.
- Various experts and skilled crewmen may now be employed.
- Players can receive Status symbol images for various achievements.

Ship Changes

- The ability to improve individual parameters of the ship has been added.
- The ship may now be equipped with special armaments.
- You may also equip the ship with equipment that will improve its characteristics.
- You now have the ability to repair your ship.
- Different types of guns can be established on the ship.
- You can now choose what types of charges would be loaded on the ship.
- You may now set the flag for your ship.
- The sails on the ship can now be changed.
- An achieved Status symbol may be displayed on the sails of your ship.

Weigh anchor, and let your sails fill with the Wind of Luck!
