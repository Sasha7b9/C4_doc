Awesome Derelict Stuff

by Jaswolf » 15 Feb 2014, 05:37

Hi,

I'd like to share with you a video of an early prototype for my next game "Awesome Derelict Stuff".

http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uzdKbbjEbhg

Under the hood:
Everything is created and drawn at run time. (the .wrld file is almost empty)

The level is randomly and procedurally generated.
When the player moves, the objects are drawn/undrawn in realtime. (inside a perimeter centered on the player)
Every object is made of a 4 by 4 geometries (plates for the floor and cubes for everything else)
You can see clearly the tile's scale at a corridor intersection(the light blue tile)

The "door" is a model (2 plates, 4 tubes, 1 hemisphere, 1 point light, 1 spot light, 4 materials(diffuse,specular,microfacet,emission, moving texture). A controller is assigned to each door. The move() function plays a sound every 10s (humming kind of sound)

Every other object has a material with a diffuse color, a specular and micro facet reflection.

The "projectile" (model) is a cube with a point light (2 moving textures, glow, emission color,specular, rigidbody controller,sound)

There's a fog space and some music.
This level has a perimeter of 4km by 4km (2.5 miles)

Every graphics options are maximized and enabled via the 31st graphics settings menu (resolution 1920x1080, anisotropic=16,antialiasing=8,...)
sync to display mode = smart.

It runs on a PC laptop (i7 4700HQ, 16 Go RAM, Nvidia GTX780M, Win8.1 64bit)
The build was compiled in optimized 32bit mode.


I'm really amazed by the general performance of the engine. Though we all already knew that
I'm really pissed of by the poor video recording quality I've achieved (I've struggled with almost every single recording software and codecs but failed to obtain a high video quality, any suggestions will be appreciated)

Nevertheless, I hope you'll enjoy it.

Re: Awesome Derelict Stuff

by Jaswolf » 16 Feb 2014, 10:08

Thank to JamesH's help, his great Animator plugin and the video recording function of the C4 Engine, I can record high quality videos directly from the engine.
And I obtained an almost good video quality on youtube.
My original mp4 video that I sent to youtube has no flaws visible. Now, I'm sure that their video reprocessing destroyed completely the black grey floor...

You can check out the new video here: http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DpdjRCZS8PA

If anyone is interested by my workflow, here it is:

- type in C4 command dialogue box : bind F1 "record Import/MyMovies/Movie -rate 30"
- hit F1 to record, hit F1 again to stop the recording
- after having installed and compiled the animator plugin : type in C4 command: "convert".
It will convert all your .seq files in .tga files
- use VirtualDub to convert all your .tga + the .wav(generated by C4) in a glorious .avi high quality file.
- use Handbreak to encode in .mp4 with H264 codec your .avi file (you'll end up with light through heavy drops in your video quality, depending on how you set up the H264 codec)

(Prepare yourself to deal with huge files during this process)
