Re: trackball kills keyboard input

by Eric Lengyel » 03 Feb 2015, 19:58

The scroll wheel on the Logitech M570 works correctly in C4 version 4.2.

The Apple Magic Mouse does not have a scroll wheel. It actually has a low-resolution touch surface, and that is not exposed through the HID manager on the Mac. There is no way for the engine to get input from this touch surface in gameplay mode, unfortunately, so you cannot use it as a scroll wheel unless you leave the engine in GUI event mode.
