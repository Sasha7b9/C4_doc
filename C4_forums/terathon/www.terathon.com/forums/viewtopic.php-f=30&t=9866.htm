Wow that's a lot of questions.

I don't want or need a team. I am an experienced programmer and to lesser extent 3D artist. At some point I may hire better artists to do some of the work, but I'd only consider that after I was complete done with the project. Posting design docs would take oomph out of the experience of actually playing the game. I have had a C4 license since 2007 when I finally realized homemade engine wasn't going to cut it, but I like to remain secretive. So it may seem like this is some typical pretentious vaporware egotrip I just decided to pretend to make last night it's an ongoing project which is closer to complete than started.

I've spent maybe 20k on various tools and services, many of which turned out to be useless. Ultimately even maya looks like I will be ditching it eventually. Otherwise budget is my labor, multiplied by several years of work. And to hire someone else to do the work I've done you'd have to pay a premium, not just my salary. A GUI heavy RPG/squad game and very complex game system would cost millions of dollars to contract out to software consultants and take years to complete, then afterwards it would cost even more to fix all the bad design, which is why games like that don't get made any more and probably never will except by someone like me who can see it through start to finish.
