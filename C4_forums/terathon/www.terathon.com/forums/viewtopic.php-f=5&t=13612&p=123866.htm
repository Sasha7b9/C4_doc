Re: Lighting problem with portals

by Eric Lengyel » 29 Mar 2014, 13:52

OK, here's what's happening. You have the infinite zone and a cylindrical subzone of that infinite zone. The floor geometry intersects the cylinder zone, so it is considered for visibility when the camera is in the cylinder zone. There's a pocket of space to the left of your portals in which the camera is in the cylinder zone, but can't see into the enclosing infinite zone because it can't see the portal that's now behind it. The light region flowing from the infinite zone into the cylinder zone starts at the portal, and no part of the floor geometry extends past the portal, so there is no intersection between the floor geometry and the light region in the subzone. That's why it isn't lit when the camera is inside that small pocket of space.

The fix for this specific problem is simple. Just move the left-facing portal to the left by one grid square. This makes the portal visible from all points inside the cylinder zone, and that pocket of space causing the problem goes away. (Do not move the right-facing portal as well because doing so would create the same problem in the other direction.)

However, there is still another problem that you may not have seen. If an object is small enough to be completely contained in that same pocket of space (and the demo game player is small enough if you get him into the right spot), then it will only be a member of the cylinder zone, and it won't be visible from camera positions in the infinite zone because the portal looking into the cylinder zone is to the right of the object. This is a result of the way subzones work. Since subzones are completely contained inside their enclosing zones, anything that is fully covered by a subzone does not get considered for membership in the enclosing zone. (But something that sticks out a little does get put in the enclosing zone as well as the subzone.) Otherwise, you would always be able to see the contents of a subzone from its enclosing zone, and this would defeat the whole purpose of having subzones. What you really want to do is have sibling zones for a level like yours so that the cylinder zone and some zone containing the corridor are at the same level of the node hierarchy. As long as these zones overlap between the portals, then any object contained in that problematic pocket of space will automatically become a member of both zones and will be visible from any camera position in either zone regardless of whether the portals can be seen.

Re: Lighting problem with portals

by Eric Lengyel » 29 Mar 2014, 20:42

cbken wrote:So it is also ok to have zones overlap at the portals. Is it correct to say that they SHOULD overlap or just that it's ok and maybe in some cases necessary?


It's normal and expected for zones to overlap at boundaries between rooms/corridors/etc. You would usually want the portals to be in the overlap region, but they aren't required to be there exactly, and the portals for each direction do not need to be in the same place. In general, in the new zoning system introduced in the version 3.x releases, zones and portals are done "loosely", and they don't need to have exact alignment (nor would you really want them to unless it was easy in some particular situation).
