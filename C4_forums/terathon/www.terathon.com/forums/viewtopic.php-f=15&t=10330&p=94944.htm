Re: Why is everything smooth?

by Eric Lengyel » 12 Feb 2011, 21:00

The Collada importer respects the normals that are written in the DAE file unless you explicitly tell it to recalculate those normals. If the smoothing groups disappeared, then it's happening during the export process.
