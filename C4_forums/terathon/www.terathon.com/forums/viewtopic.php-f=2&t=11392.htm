Perpetual updates are back!

by Eric Lengyel » 07 Dec 2011, 19:35

We have brought back perpetual updates for all C4 Engine licensees! All current and new license holders for all editions of the engine will now receive all updates to the engine for the rest of their lives at no additional cost.

We understand that this may cause licensees who have already paid for a renewal to think they have wasted some money. We don't want that to be the case, and we are willing to make up for it. If you have paid for a one-year renewal on your license, then we are offering the following three options:

1) You can apply the amount that you paid for a renewal toward a license upgrade. This means that a previously renewed basic license can be upgraded to standard for $100 and a previously renewed standard license can be upgraded to professional for $350. If you would like to exercise this option, please submit an upgrade order in your account. The order page will state the ordinary full amount of the upgrade, but we will deduct your previous renewal charge from the amount you actually get charged, and this will be reflected on your receipt. There is no time limit for this option. Upgrade whenever you like, and we will apply the credit.

2) You can apply the amount that you paid for a renewal toward a future iOS or Android license. C4 is likely to support these platforms in 2012. There is no time limit for this option. Purchase an iOS or Android license whenever you like after they become available, and we will apply the credit.

3) If you are in the US or Canada, you can ask us for a goodie box containing all of the following items, with a total retail value of $170: A copy of the book Mathematics for 3D Game Programming and Computer Graphics, 3rd Ed., a copy of the book Game Engine Gems 2, a C4 Engine T-shirt, and a C4 Engine wall poster. This option cannot be guaranteed to exist forever because supplies may run out.

by Eric Lengyel » 07 Dec 2011, 23:19

Advoc wrote:EDIT: So a previously non-renewed Standard license can be upgraded to a Pro license for $450, even though the new standard price is 250?


Yes, we will take all previous amounts paid into account, and the total amount you pay over all time will not exceed the current price for a pro license. So if you previously paid $350 for a standard license, you can upgrade to the pro license for $450 when you purchase directly through our website. The upgrade option in your account may show a higher amount, but rest assured that you will not be overcharged.
