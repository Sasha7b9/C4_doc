ethanc wrote:tree/Maple/Leaves-colr
tree/Maple/Leaves-glos-xmit
tree/Maple/Leaves-diff
tree/Maple/Leaves-nrml


These are stored in Data/Mangler.pak.

ethanc wrote:I can "kinda" figure out that the "nrml" is obviously the normalmap and possible the "diff" is the intended diffuse, but I'm at a loss as to what to do with the others.


Diff is the ordinary diffuse map, nrml is the normal map, glos-xmit contains a gloss factor and a transmission factor, and colr contains extra info about the colors of the leaves. The glos-xmit and colr textures are special for the maple tree and don't need to be included in other shaders.

Re: Wierd Lighting on Foliage

by Javier Moya » 28 Feb 2010, 07:26

You can see here (my online portfolio) some more info about the maple leaves texture set. There are just a couple of 'non standart' textures, the light transmission map and the color mask map. The light transmission map contains info about how much light passes through the elements shown in the texture, and it involves things like the thickness of the elements and the shadows projected between those elements (some elements may be above others). Just to explain it a little bit more, the color mask (as I named it in my portfolio) is just grayscale info (0 to 1) which is used as an interpolation value between two colors for getting the leaves color. This way you can make tree variations easily and get the classic look where each leaf has its own color tone (of course controlling the overall look).
The 'glos-xmit thing' is just the gloss texture with the light transission info in another channel. As those maps are grayscale maps, you can have both in one texture and import them as luminance-alpha. This way you can have all the information needed in one file instead of two.

Hope it helps.
