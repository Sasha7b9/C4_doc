Very good to see another logo contest in progress.

Just to emphasize some of the earlier general points about the logo design, it helps if a logo will work well on a light or dark background, or comes in both light and dark background versions, since this was a bit of an issue with the old logo.

Also, although raster graphics designed in Photoshop look cool on the screen, it would be awesome if the winning logo were created as vector graphics, simply so it could be scaled up or down as needed, and to make printing easier and more precise.
