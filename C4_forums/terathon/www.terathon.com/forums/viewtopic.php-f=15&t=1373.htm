OK, that seems easy enough. There will be an animation for the weapon model that bobs it up and down, that is played when the player is running. When the player stops, that animation will be faded off, and the weapon will then smoothly stop bobbing up and down.

So how would aiming a sniper rifle work? Would there be an animation for the weapon, that translates the weapon from its normal origin up and to the left a bit, so it would be in front of the soldier's head?

Also, how would one create an animation for when the soldier is changing the cartridge in a weapon? From what I've seen in other games, the "change ammo cartridge" animation for the weapon would first tilt the weapon up at an angle, then tilt it slightly to the right, while the soldier reaches over with his left hand, pulls out the old cartridge, and then reaches down into an ammo belt for another cartridge, finally plugging in the new cartridge. Would the soldier's left arm movements have to be temporarily detached from the weapon during this sequence?

It seems like it would be difficult to synchronize the weapon animation with the soldier's arm and hand animations, so I'm curious how you think this should all work.

by KingJehu » 13 Nov 2006, 13:39

make human is pretty useful actually, and makes what would be considered as a medium poly model, and using it as a base can help make sure everything stays in proportion.

most artist start with a med poly model. once they are happy with the design, they then create a low poly model from the med poly.
when the low poly is finished, they then create a super high poly model from the med poly one.
then they use the low and high poly models to create normal maps.

seems involved, but it works pretty well.
