Forum ranks updated

by Eric Lengyel » 13 Apr 2007, 16:32

We've added new rank icons to these forums. Up to 4 plasma guns will be displayed beneath your name based on the number of posts that you've made. The crossover points are 50, 200, and 400 posts.

There is also a new rank called "Community Leader" for which a proton cannon icon is displayed beneath your name. This rank has initially been bestowed upon three members of the community: Huidafa, Frank Skilton, and Jarrod1937. If you would like to nominate additional recipients of this rank, please do so in this topic.
