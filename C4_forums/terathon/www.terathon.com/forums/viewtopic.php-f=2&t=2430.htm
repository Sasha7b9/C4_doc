by Eric Lengyel » 13 May 2007, 22:24

Argh. I forgot to strip the rendering options out of the CFG file so that the engine would choose some defaults based on the graphics hardware. I've uploaded a new demo that should work right. I'm going to have to put something in that will prevent high-quality motion blur from being rendered on the Radeon 9x00 series.

If you want to determine what kind of hardware you're running on from code, you can do this:

Code: Select all
const GraphicsCapabilities *caps = TheGraphicsMgr->GetCapabilities();
unsigned long gpuType = caps->hardwareType;
unsigned long gpuGen = caps->hardwareGeneration;


The hardware type can be kHardwareNV or kHardwareATI (or kHardwareUnknown), and the generation is as follows:

Generation = 0: Nvidia 5x00 or ATI 9x00
Generation = 1: Nvidia 6x00 or ATI Xx00
Generation = 2: Nvidia 7x00 or ATI X1x00
Generation = 3: Nvidia 8x00

by Eric Lengyel » 14 May 2007, 15:22

Jason wrote:Are framebuffer objects new?


Framebuffer objects (FBOs) are about two years old. The OpenGL community made a big deal about how great they were and how all vendors will support them right away, but Nvidia is the only vendor to fully support them so far. ATI and Apple are way behind and don't seem to care that FBOs still can't be used for their intended purposes: a replacement for pbuffers.
