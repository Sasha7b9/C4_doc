Re: Wind of Luck - a pirate MMO game

by Trazzy » 20 Jul 2013, 04:45

a2k wrote:Nice. But what happened to the UI that was shaped like a ship? Did the design not match the shape any more?


As we wrote earlier the project had undergone significant changes. Now it will be two games, first one, Wind of Luck: Arena, and the second, Wind of Luck: Pirates.
The most significant change is that both games will be free-to-play. WoL: Arena is about naval battles in separate locations (maps) where you will gain experience after each battle and will have an opportunity to buy ships of a higher level.
WoL: Pirates will be a full-fledged MMO, there will be quests, fractions, a huge world, etc. where you will be living a life of a real pirate!

And answering to your question, there will be different UIs in these games. On this particular video, you will see the user interface for Wind of Luck: Arena.
