After posting a few hours later I got something like transparency in light pass. It wasn´t so hard to implement. Due to the fact that light is accumulated in the light pass I got some brightness where two transparency geometries are meet.
So the question is how could I avoid the brightness. I don´t expect step by step solution but a few ideas were nice.

Very short explanation how I achieved transparency:
On top of solid rendering I render in the ambient pass again with alpha blending enabled and then in light pass with all the light that beeing rendered in the solid rendering pass. In light pass I blend with "BlendState(kBlendSourceAlpha, kBlendOne)"

Re: Alpha Output in Light Pass

by miken » 17 May 2013, 13:57

Jack wrote:I wonder how the other engines do transparency with multiple lights. Since I got rid of stencil shadows I can maybe use stencil buffer to mask allready written geomety.


Typically you just render the object once and send all of the lights affecting it to the fragment shader. On modern hardware you can just send an array of lights and loop over it. On older hardware you might have a restriction of say up to 4 or 8 lights affecting one object, and you generate shader permutations for each combination of lights. Not meaning to speak badly, but I believe both of these methods are probably impossible/very difficult to achieve with C4's shader system, although you might be able to send the light data in as user material parameters and do the lighting yourself, but your shader graph is going to be quite large

Re: Alpha Output in Light Pass

by Jack » 20 May 2013, 06:10

First of all there are some restrictions. The important one ist that you can have only one
directional light (either depth, landscape or basic infinite light). You can also have several point lights, spot lights and cube lights. However you get best results when only one light is used.

Transparent geometries are rendered on top of solid rendering after ambient and light passes as I said in the first post. You need to remember the lights that were unsed to render solid geometries and also the shadow data. Ambient pass needs to be extended in order to allow directional lighting. Now all transparent geometries are renderd with the directional light (and shadow data) in ambient pass. Other light types are rendered as usual in the light pass.
