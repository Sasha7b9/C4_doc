Re: Sound Issues, possibly realtek audio?

by Twisted Helix » 10 Dec 2010, 10:45

Can you define "distortion" ? There are different types. Does it seem to start becoming "grungy" (dont worry ... using words to describe sound is difficult at best ) ?

Basically a fact of digital audio is that the lower the volume the less bits there are to construct the wave form, and so you end up with the wave form becoming stepped, and this angular form causes "distortion", which is similar to the harmonics generated by a square wave (which is also what happens when you overload a digital system, the top gets chopped off the wave form producing a square wave shape).

The difference between the types of distortion is subtle, but its certainly different ... the difficulty is in describing the difference. Does the distortion sound "on top" of the signal ... or does it sound "inside" the signal ? In other words is it a raspy sound that obliterates the sound, or is it a kind of gritty grainyness that seems to be inside the sound ?

Regards issues with realtek onboard sound ... I have had a lot in the past. But then I have had a lot of issues with Creative cards as well. I am at a bit of a loss as to what constitutes a decent sound card for games to be honest, as I simply cannot (from the perspective of a sound engineer) rate any of them that I have tried, above awful. It really seems to be one area that is a gaping hole in the hardware side of gaming, and I would be grateful if anyone has any decent solution.
