Those machines are actually vehicles - Ships to be exact much smaller than the capitol ships. We've closed off that world until 1.6 since with the terrain changes and so forth we'll probably end up having to re-do a lot of it, but in regards to the ships hull:

That's an Omicron (OSI) Ship so it, like everything Omicron is meant to be really shiny, really bright, really benevolent looking. The hull is actually normally white but the sun where those videos were shot emits soft orange/yellow light so it's kinda hard to tell I think. I might look for a way of conveying that it's supposed to be white while still getting the color we want sometime in the future.

The other factions have much more of a utilitarian look such as the one i've included below. The exterior of the station is a good example of the grit and wear-and-tear associated with the third faction called DC. The goal is to have greatly different factions rather than carbon copies with different names.
