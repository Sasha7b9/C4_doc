Re: Racing game

by cbken » 07 Jan 2013, 14:51

The terrain is made in a 3rd party 3D app and the road is part of it. Splines for the road shape and then lofted and terrain built from there. I also did a test with just having the road itself plus a bit on each side imported and build a voxel terrain around it but it was taking a lot of time trying to shape the terrain getting it to blend in nicely. In the end I was not happy with the result and decided it's just as easy to build the whole terrain outside of C4 and it gives me more control over it.

The car is using a custom physics system with ray casts for the wheels. This is still a work in progress.
