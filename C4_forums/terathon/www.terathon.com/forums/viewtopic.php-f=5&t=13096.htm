Re: Outdoor Lights, Zones and Portals

by Eric Lengyel » 04 Aug 2013, 18:06

I'll summarize a few important properties of infinite lights in the new zoning system:

* The physical position of an infinite light is not used when determining which zone it's a member of. The light is placed in its parent zone in the transform tree (as seen in the scene graph viewport), and it shines through any portals facing it to reach other zones.

* The light can be forced to be a member of all subzones up to and including zones at the forced subzone depth that you specify. We use this a lot in outdoor levels to put the light in a bunch of zones without using portals.

* There is a double-secret feature in version 3.0 that lets you make the light a member of an exact set of zones. You can add the %member connector to the light and then connect it to a Connection Marker that is itself connected to any number of other zones. This will make the light a member of those zones in addition to the light's parent zone and any forced subzones.

* In all cases, the light can flow from any zone its a member of to other zones through a sequence of portals.

Zone::AddNewSubzone() is an undocumented internal function that you shouldn't be calling (and it has been made private in the next release so you can't call it by accident). You need to call AddNewSubnode() instead. With the code above, the second-level zone is never actually added to the scene.
