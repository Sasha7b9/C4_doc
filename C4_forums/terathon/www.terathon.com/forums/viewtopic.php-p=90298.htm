Beginner's Guide in Registered Access Area

by Eric Lengyel » 21 Oct 2010, 07:22

The Beginner's Guide to the C4 Engine can now be purchased from your account page in the registered access area through either PayPal or Google Checkout.

If you have already purchased The Guide and the email address you used for your purchase matches the email address on your C4 license, then you should see a download link on your account page. If you used a different email address (or had Google Checkout generate an address), then you'll need to send the following information to us to get your download link activated:

* Your name.
* Your C4 license number (not your download key).
* If you paid through a PayPal account under a business name, then include that name as well.

Please send this information to guide@terathon.com. If you send it to a different address, don't expect anything to happen.

Niteen Hatle wrote:Can't I buy this book directly with my credit card? Paypal and Google Checkout don't work for me. I've had problems with these two services while buying C4 Engine license.

If both Paypal AND Google reject your card, it's probably something as mundane as typing "123 Mystreet, #2" instead of "123 Mystreet, Unit 2" (or whatever is on file). It's retarded I know, but that's just how it is.

Another possibility is that the card has been flagged for "suspicious activity" - which is done for your own protection when there's a lot of purchases in a short time span being posted through different payment gateways. You'll have to contact the issuing bank to have it corrected. Honestly, you're much better off not providing your details directly to each website each time you buy something anyway..
