Build 144 Bonus Feature

by Eric Lengyel » 10 Nov 2007, 20:39

A new feature for quickly playing a level has been added to the World Editor in build 144. Download both Code.zip and Data.zip to get it. The World menu now has a "Save and Play World" command with shortcut Ctrl-P.
