Re: iOS devices

by Benjamin Dobell » 29 Jan 2015, 09:38

Rastar wrote:I always thought that the nicest mobile games were the ones that didn't simply translate the usual desktop/console metaphors to the smaller device (like those two joystick circles you often see in the bottom corners), but those that made good use of the specific possibilities for interaction (holding the device like a wheel in racing games, swiping to throw stuff at people etc).


Agreed. However, it's generally a matter of game design, and realising that the same desktop/console gaming experience just won't be fun on a touch screen device. FPS (unfortunately for Eric / the 31st) are the hardest games to translate and to do a really good job involves completely changing the game (i.e. turning it into a rail shooter). Given the difficulty associated, this is why a lot of games simply end up taking the on-screen joystick approach - I'd expect that if the 31st is to be released on iOS then that will be the approach taken.

by Eric Lengyel » 29 Jan 2015, 16:58

Dalus wrote:Is it too early to inquire about pricing? I'm definitely interested in this.


The iOS license is going to be $300, and we'll begin taking pre-orders in a few days. Everyone who already pre-ordered as part of the OpenGEX campaign on Indiegogo, as well as those who make new pre-orders, will get immediate access to the development build for iOS. The first development build will support only those devices with an A7 or A8 processor, and then support for all devices capable of OpenGL ES 2.0 will follow.
