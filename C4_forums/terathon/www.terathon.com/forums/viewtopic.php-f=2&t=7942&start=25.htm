Hmm, I have an idea that an artist might like to try out:

http://www.animatedengines.com/otto.shtml

If you just included the crankshaft and the cog (plus the C bit connected to the cog) you could make a C from the bit connected to the cog and either a backwards 4 using the crankshaft or a separate 4 connected by some other means. If you make the logo in Adobe Illustration or something similar it can easily be rescaled. You could then have two type of animations, one the simplified cartoon version that just contains the cog and crankshaft rotating. Then you could have a more complex version which would make an awesome 3D intro video for C4.

By that I mean the video would start outside a 3D animated internal combustion engine and the camera could zoom in through the motor to the crankshaft where the C4 logo would be found. Obviously then you can freeze the animation when the C is upright and say "Powered by the C4 Game Engine".

I know that sounds like a lot, I'm not artist or I would give it a go myself but I really feel that the C4 Engine deserves a professional well thought out logo and intro video.

Are there any artists or 3D modellers here willing to give it a shot?
