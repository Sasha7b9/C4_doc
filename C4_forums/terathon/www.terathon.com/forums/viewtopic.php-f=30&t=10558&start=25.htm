Re: Gremlin Invasion

by Jesse Meyer » 21 Mar 2011, 12:03

What has been said about shadows is an explanation of how, at a physical level, they work. In reality, ambient light is indirect light, even if that light is from the primary light source - because light bounces. When we are dealing with atmosphere, the ambient light is generally the color of the sky, mix in (type)clouds and any close proximity objects, and the more reflective the object, the greater the impact.

All that to say, is to remember what you want your game to look like. If you're aiming for a comic book style, get some comic books and as best you can, mimic their result. Things do not have to be realistic in order for it to look good.
