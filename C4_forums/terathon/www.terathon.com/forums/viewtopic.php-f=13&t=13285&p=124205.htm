BORODA wrote:And my project and 2 years of my work have recently gone to hell because my partner left and took the other engine (no ads) license with him ...Now I'm gonna start all over. Alone. With a new engine.


Its the sad truth that has burned many of us over time. And probably a huge reason its so hard to get a team going. Its extremely hard to be trusting when its so easy for anyone walk away with everyone's hard work. BUT, that is for another topic.

Back on Topic:
In all honesty, I "KNOW" that Eric will deliver on the 31st, so that is why I am totally not concerned about the time it gets delivered.
There are also several reasons why:

1. The higher the quality of the 31st, the higher the quality that any other developer's C4 Engine based game will also have access to. We get the same engine. As a Pro developer, we get access to the complete source and the assets as well. What better way to improve the engine than to have to go thru the process that all of us pretty much have to go thru on the way.

2. (derivative of #1) I am very much looking forward to things like: Hardware Instancing, Atmospheric Shaders etc... because I am pretty sure, that the 31st will need them to really show off the engine. (as we have already discussed people like to see the eye candy). I am not 100% sure they will be in the 31st, but I am really really hoping they will make it in!

So, really, I don't mind waiting longer. I realize tho, it is a going to be a wait.
