Gremlin Invasion

by smittix » 18 Mar 2011, 02:21

I'd like to officially announce Gremlin Invasion, an overhead shooter I've been working on with another member (ska) of the C4 community for the last few months. You play as a mercenary who has been hired to invade a planet infested with gremlins and wipe them out, using their souls as currency to buy new weapons along the way. The first level of this game has been completed and extensively play-tested, and will be released as a demo within a week from now. The game is currently in a stable, playable state and aside from the UI, is a very good example of what the final product will be like. Let me know what you think, and as soon as the demo is available it will be posted here.

Official Site: http://cssquaredgames.com/game.php?id=2

Controls:
W, A, S, D to move
Space to Jump
Mouse 1 to shoot
Mouse 2 to throw grenade
1 - 6 Switch weapons
R to reload
F to use Medkit
E to Kick

PC Demo: http://www.cssquaredgames.com/GremlinInvasion.rar or http://www.megaupload.com/?d=DIY32DUY

Re: Gremlin Invasion

by Jesse Meyer » 18 Mar 2011, 11:49

Good to hear your thoughts on the crits, ska.

To add my perspective : The larger issue with the graphics isn't really 'the graphics', but user perception. There is a strange phenomenon that occurs when people view something as 'consistent' or 'obviously intentional, with skill' - they feel good. It's helpful to make it that simple.

When things come across as a bit sloppy or inconsistent, the pattern analyzer sections of our brains go haywire and people don't feel good. I'm sure you've experienced this - like watching a movie with obvious green screen. Since most people look for quick and easy satisfaction, this issue is one of the main reasons people would refuse to buy a product, or even try it due to 'grafix sux lol', even if gameplay is stellar.

This may be hard to absorb, and it probably is. I've seen great titles suffer from this issue, and it breaks me a little bit everytime it does.

Hope to see more of this soon!

Re: Gremlin Invasion

by Grognard » 20 Mar 2011, 18:47

wtblife wrote:Does ambient lighting affect shadows? In this case I would think it'd be too much light making stuff so bright when it isn't hit by a shadow... I'm like trying to be coherent here and it's rough, super dizzy and nauseous, rarely get migraines this bad...


There's no such thing as a shadow, there's only absence of light.

If you have only one light and your ambient is very low everything will look dead black when it's in shadow.

For some scenes that's appropriate, like exploring in a creepy dungeon. For outside scenes it's going to look totally wrong so you need to either crank up ambient when you are outside or have some more complex lighting system.

It's kind of hard to explain to people, but the issue is lighting not the engine. I could post screenshots to show shadows can be very light in C4 but I am too lazy.
