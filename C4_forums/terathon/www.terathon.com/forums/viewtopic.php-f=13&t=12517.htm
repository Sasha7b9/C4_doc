Re: 3DConnexion Mice

by mschifter » 03 Jan 2013, 12:20

Hi Davaris,

I recently bought my $100 space navigator 3d connexion mouse for blender use. It is a 6 dof (rotation xyz and displacement xyz) with two buttons, one for resetting the view. It only manipulates the camera but it is sensitive enough to get those subtle angles quickly. I can operate it with my southpaw with a natural feel. The company is now owned by Logitech. I would say it is not a significant timesaver for me -- I usually make more violent camera maneuvers with my mouse and keyboard, and that is quicker than the pressure-based 3d connexion device. The company makes some claim about improved efficiency when using it, but I disagree. If I was a pro artist, I would consider the space explorer and ignore anything I said here.

I also have an old intuos tablet with the air-brush option. It was great for texture work using painter and paint shop pro. I never used it in a modeling environment, however.

Hope this helps,

Matt

I was expecting hi-performance and hi-precision camera control, and hi-precision is certainly there.

The original spaceball was around 15,000 bucks! It was a strain-gauge (doesn't move measurably, but senses pressure) device for expensive machines from the early 90s.
