Beginner's Guide, Second Edition, now available

by Eric Lengyel » 23 Jun 2012, 03:44

The second edition of The Beginner's Guide to the C4 Engine is now available for electronic download to those who purchased the first edition of the book. It can be downloaded from your C4 Engine account page.

A separate announcement will be made when the authors have made new copies of the second edition available for purchase. At this time, the second edition is only available to people who already purchased the first edition. New purchases of the second edition cannot yet be made.
