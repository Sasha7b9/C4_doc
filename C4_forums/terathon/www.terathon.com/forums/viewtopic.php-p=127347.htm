by patlecat » 08 Jan 2015, 10:46

First time that I tried the Demo levels of 4.1 and with my new hardware and it was impressive. I maxed all the features/options and played at 1920x1080. The Island level was the heaviest to chew on for my machine and I got between ~50 - ~130fps out of it. The more trees are in the view and/or the further the view distance the lower the fps. All other levels gave me at least between ~130 - ~180 fps often peaking at above 200. And the Physics level never dropped below 200fps often being around 250 - 300fps no matter how many barrels were flying around. Compared to my old laptop this is like a quantum leap LOL

My specs:
  • intel i7 4710HG
  • 16GB 1866 RAM
  • Micron SSD
  • nVidia GTX-980M with 4GB DDR5
  • Windows 8.1 64bit
