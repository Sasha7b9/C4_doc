Gremlin Invasion: Survivor

by smittix » 10 Mar 2012, 01:55

While still midway through the development of our campaign, Gremlin Invasion, we decided to take a little time and make a multiplayer offshoot called Gremlin Invasion: Survivor. With 8 different levels to choose from and up to 8 players, you'll play together in a survival-style mission to blast away hordes of gremlins that have come to take you out. You'll have access only to the weapons and supplies dropped throughout the mission, so use your health and ammo wisely - all you have to do is survive!

We wanted to keep this game under wraps until completion, and it's finally complete and ready to publish. We are currently waiting for the game to be approved on Desura, and which time it will be for sale for $4.99. To be clear, this is a separate game from Gremlin Invasion. Gremlin Invasion will be a long single player campaign, whereas Gremlin Invasion: Survivor is a collection of short (5 - 8 minute) long survival-style matches. Gremlin Invasion is still a ways off, but we're still working hard at it and plan to have it ready later this year.
