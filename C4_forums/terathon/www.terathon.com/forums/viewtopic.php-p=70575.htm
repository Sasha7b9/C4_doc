The problem with many of those logo's, C22 included, is they are too complex. The best and most well known logos/brands are very simple, clear and concise.

Having a complex logo doesn't help when doing brochures, business cards etc either - particularly if you need to use your logo somewhere that it must be small. Sure rendered logo's look nice, but you are better off leaving the rendering to your screenshots and demo's, and having a simple logo.

Just look at other engines like Garage Games or Unity. Simple, easily recognizable.
