Quest for Jaeger IIV

by eborenst » 08 Mar 2010, 19:07



Hi all

Over the past 5 weeks or so, a bunch of us here at WPI have been making game prototypes in C4 as part of a pair of game dev classes. I don't know if gogo is planning to post the game list, but my group wanted to share our game with the forums. You can find our website here: http://sites.google.com/site/questforjaegeriiv/.

A direct link to the playable download is here: http://www.elliotb.com/Downloads/Games/Quest_For_Jaeger_RC.rar.

As I said, we had about 5 weeks to go from starting design to pitching our finished prototype. The actual development period was about 2 weeks long. Please enjoy, and we welcome any feedback!

Re: Quest for Jaeger IIV

by Mr. Bowser » 08 Mar 2010, 22:39

Just played through and beat it, it was fun !

Specific things I liked:
I liked how each box would gradually light up, neat little effect.
The elevator music while in game was funny, reminded me of Portal (in the fact there's a bit of fun little music just playing in the background)
You have to go and collect the pieces, not sure why I liked this bit (not that there's a reason to dislike it per-say) but I enjoyed the collecting.

Minor problem(s) I had:
Had a bit of trouble at times shooting the start nodes to set their color when I was doing a ground puzzle, as I was usually standing right next to it and the "paint" would hit to the bottom right of it (so I'd have to manually adjust).
Again its a minor problem but I just thought I'd let you know.

Suggestions:
More levels!
Perhaps puzzles that go on the floor & wall?
Perhaps a story, give me motivation, why am I going around collecting panels and sticking them together? (I figured I might as well add this to the list as it's a wishlist)
Perhaps have a more "interesting" environment? I mean you could just simply add plants to the levels or something .
Perhaps more difficult puzzles? I'm not usually super good at puzzle games but the only puzzle I truly had problems with was the first puzzle to use both red & green. Though be nice and make sure to include levels with varying levels of difficulty, like I said I'm not usually great at puzzle games!

All in all I enjoyed it, and looking forward to future updates .
