Fence placing tool

by cbken » 30 Dec 2012, 17:08

I have made my first custom C4 tool and although it's probably not anything exciting to the rest of you, it will be an enormous time saver for me. I made a fence placing tool! That's right

This tool will allow for placing fence or wall pieces on the ground and it will automatically line it up with the ground and if you click on an already placed fence piece it will line it up with it so you get a continuous fence. When testing various level things I quickly realized that building up a fence or a wall was a huge time sink. Trying to move and rotate pieces so they lined up with both ground and another piece was extremely tedious. So I had to automate it somehow.

To avoid modifying the engine code I simply made a copy of the Worlds page, added my own code to it and made a custom plugin of it. When a fence instance is placed it does the right thing. Now I can lay out pieces on the terrain in a fraction of the time it would take to manually place and line them up.

I also again want to say that writing code on top of the C4 Engine is a real joy because of how incredibly well designed it is.

Re: Fence placing tool

by cbken » 03 Jan 2013, 21:39

MACK wrote:Yea, this is really excellent.
Are all your pieces required to sort of be cuboid?
ie, how do you tell your tool how to align the "pieces" of fence?


Not necessarily. Any shape could work with some code changes but it's tailored to fences or walls since that's what I needed. I add a custom property to fence pieces and that property both marks it as a fence piece and also holds the length of the piece. The pivot point of a piece is at one end of it and then the next piece is placed at piece length from the pivot in the direction of the piece.
