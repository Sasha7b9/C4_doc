Re: Version 2.9 Beta Now Available

by Eric Lengyel » 11 May 2012, 21:03

Jesse Meyer wrote:There is an issue I've found with imposters that cannot be shown in a screenshot. On ONLY the bottom right quadrant of the screen space, when shifting the camera quickly seems to render only parts of the imposters on the frame at a time. Once the camera stops moving it clears up.

EDIT :: ACTUALLY - it only happens if the imposter is behind the player's weapon model in first person camera.


That's being caused by the motion blur. A higher-quality algorithm is used on tiles touched by the weapon so that the weapon doesn't blur into the background. You're seeing the boundary between the low-quality and high-quality blurring algorithms. I've been meaning to do something about this, but it's kind of low priority.
