Re: Sprung Arch(itecture)

by MachineryFrank » 30 Dec 2009, 07:02

Yes, like Amo said, you can do whatever you want except selling, lending or giving away as texture/model packs.

And you are right, not all packs have closed meshes. Most packs from other artists come with opened meshes. Sometimes I find the time to close them, sometimes not. My own packs are closed. But maybe we dont have to care about this detail in the future when we get shadow mapping instead of stencils in C4.

We will surely continue to support Collada. C4 and Shiva reads Collada. T3D could theoretically use it as well but I have bad experiences so far, that is why we still support DTS for this engine. Unity3d can read it but just like T3D it works better with its favourite format (FBX). Gamestudio can read our FBX files well and some other engines need .x files. Besides that we support Milkshape and Ultimate Unwrap 3d. So our customers can export to tons of other formats from there.
