by MACK » 06 Jan 2014, 12:06

There is a file included with the assets called "_License_Terms" it comes in .odt and .txt.

It is the legal document that gives the exact details.

Here's some excerpts from it: (work refers to the book, and all associated assets, also... please note, some parts of the work are covered under separate license, such as the easing equations and the Dexsoft models)

You may use the source code confidentially for commercial or non-commercial use, provided you adhere to the limitations herein and abide by the agreement's terms.


Provided that you agree and comply with the terms herein, you may use, copy, modify, merge, distribute, sublicense, and/or sell copies of the Software only as binaries (not human readable form) to anyone even if they have not purchased the work, and to permit persons to whom the Binary is furnished to do so, subject to the conditions herein.


A significant requirement is that you may not redistribute "source" code at all.

There are many other requirements so you would have to read the whole thing, but the basic idea is that you can use the source code and sell "games" commercially using the source code, provided you don't release the source and you abide by the other terms.
Make sure you get a separate license for the Dexsoft Assets, and permission for any other assets that have a separate license (if any).

But, as I am not an attorney, you would have to discuss with them for an actual "legal" opinion.

If you make a million dollar space blaster, we would not mind if you send us a donation...

panz3r wrote:Thank you. Well this is a bit of a problem in the eventuality you would want to sell the game_sourcecode/business to another company.


Just as the C4 Engine is not transferable... neither is the source code to the book.
So, if you were to sell your "source", you would need that other company to also purchase a C4 License.
In the eventuality that you ever develop a game that uses our source, and is worthy to be sold to another company, you can always negotiate with Macktek, LLC for additional rights.

But it would be pretty trivial for that other company to buy a license to the C4 Engine, and then to buy the book from us (if it were still available).
