Re: Wind of Luck - a pirate MMO game

by Trazzy » 25 Oct 2014, 03:30

Update 0.6.6
Ladies and gentlemen of fortune on high seas!
We’re excited to announce that the game is moving onto the Pre-release phase, and the first step to it is Update 0.6.6.
The game now has 18 Specialists that would make your ship even more dangerous and awesome!
You will be able to hire Specialists in the Tavern on Shipyard. After the Specialist is hired, he will be available in the list to be accepted on the ship. Different ships can hold different number of specialist, up to three. As a general rule of thumb, Tier 1-3 ships can hold 1 Specialist only, for Tiers 4-5 there are 2 Specialist slots, and Tiers 6-9 can take 3 Specialists. Note, that it’s not possible to have two specialists with same trade on the ship (i.e. you can’t have 2 Gunners, or a Gunner and a Seasoned Gunner on a ship at the same time).
Here is a list of Specialists and their abilities:


Another precious tool o’trade, Harpoon cannon, is added in this Update too. These pieces of master craft fires extra-sharp and heavy harpoons with heavy anchor on a long rope attached. It will impale the enemy board and help you to momentarily stop the sneaky enemies that are sailing too fast or maneuver too quick. You can buy Harpoon cannons in Store/Special.
There are 12 Harpoon cannons available divided into 3 groups that would correspond with ship tiers. Harpoon cannons will differ with a range of firing, recharge time, quantity of charges and the amount of time they would be able to stop the enemy ship.


Come and have fun in the game!

Ahoy mateys!
The long-awaited Update 0.8.0. aka "Big battles" is released!

Update notes:
• Large battles, 9 vs 9 players for all tiers
• AI players are added and ready to join the battle if needed.
• Mine barrels are reworked, effectiveness for medium and large ones reduced
• Descriptions of cannon presets are corrected for all ships
• Fixed a lot of typos
• Nearly all texts were adapted to GUI limits (mostly for non-English versions)
• Mini-maps are changed and now are more precise.
• Added a special icon (arrow) for player’s ship on mini-map
• Reworked player names visibility - now enemy names are visible from afar, but not through obstacles
• Fixed a bug when ship wasn’t appearing on the shipyard after the end of battle if player left that battle before battle ended.
• Fixed the bug when battle didn’t finish properly after defeating all ships in enemy fleet
• Tutorials are working properly again
• Damage decals are added, now marks of hull damage are displayed on the ships.
• “Hit by Harpoon” indicator is added on the battle GUI
• Some GUI tweaking
• A couple of new hot keys added: for hide/show Battle GUI, for enable/disable targeting.
