Re: what am i doing wrong for acoustic space here

by Eric Lengyel » 25 Apr 2013, 21:29

In the file that you sent me, there were two reasons why you could hear the reverb outside:

1) The acoustics space was in the outside zone, not in the inside zone as in the screenshot above. You need to move the acoustics space into the inside zone by selecting the Move tool (shortcut: 2 key) and dragging it to the box zone in the scene graph viewport.
2) Both zones were connected to the acoustics space through their %acoustics connectors. This causes each zone to use the same acoustics space no matter where it is. You need to select the infinite zone, choose the connect tool (shortcut: 5 key), select the %acoustics connector, and type Ctrl-U to unconnect it (this is the Unconnect Node command under the Node menu).

Re: what am i doing wrong for acoustic space here

by Eric Lengyel » 15 May 2013, 19:06

It could be that the acoustics space is just way to big. The physical size of the acoustics space defines the generic "room size" for the reverberation, so a really big space would cause the reverberation to be very quiet, possibly inaudible. The acoustics space does not need to cover the actual volume in which you'll hear sounds.
