by MACK » 14 May 2013, 03:11

Hello Ska,

yea, i guess my usage of sketchy is "sketchy" hehe , but overall, its a really good video.

Please take these minor criticism as simply ways to improve your goal:

@17 secs there is some interesting video that shows a sort of black object melting / forming... its odd and hard to visualize in the distance. There is something grayish that moves upward on the "body" of this thing, again... no way to know what that is... its sort of "sketchy" as to what is going on there. Perhaps that is the goal?

@32 secs there is a cool scene of a book, but the lighting is "jittery"

@40 secs your character animates abruptly to the left without any transitions. Consider adding some sort of transitions when switching directions.

Also the video does not really explain the "game play" aspect. Its really has great emotional tone, but nothing explains what the player is supposed to do "in game". So, it seems as if we are watching baked scenes of incredible art, without the critical parts of how the player does what they need to do.

Do you have a narrator ? You might consider a woman with a Brazilian Accent speaking English and narrating the story as she goes thru the scenes.
