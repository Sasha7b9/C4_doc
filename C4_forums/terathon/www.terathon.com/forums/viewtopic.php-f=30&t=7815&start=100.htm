Re: Physics video of the day

by jwatte » 26 Aug 2009, 01:01

Regarding "joining" breakable objects: What I've done in other engines is set the two overlapping shapes to not detect collisions against each other, and join them with a breakable, fixed joint. At the point where they break, I remove the joint, and set them collidable with each other. If they are penetrating at that point, they'll usually fly apart with great force, so sometimes you might be better off not turning off the pairwise exclusion, and/or adding a slight separating force and turning off the exclusion when they don't intersect during one step.

Regarding the movie: I can't play it without bad pauses (three of them) on my laptop (2.0 GHz Core Duo, GeForce 7400 Go). Was it encoded using some high end codec in high resolution? Or are the bad pauses part of the Fraps capture process?

For recording screen demos these days, you really need a Black Magic Intensity Pro, which is a HDMI + RGB PCI Express capture card for $199. It'll capture not only PC video but also PlayStation and Xbox output If you want to capture to a laptop, there's another option from Matrox which connects via an ExpressCard slot but it's more like $499 I think. (Has a better break-out box, though, and can also be had with a regular PCI express card)
