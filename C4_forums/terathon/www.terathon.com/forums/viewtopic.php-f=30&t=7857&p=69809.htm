Rhys wrote:I dont quite understand the difference between ambient shader and light shader. Im guessing ambient is when there is no light cast on the object or something to that effect?


Everything always gets rendered with the ambient shader. Then the light shader is applied once for each light that's hitting an object. There's some more information here:
