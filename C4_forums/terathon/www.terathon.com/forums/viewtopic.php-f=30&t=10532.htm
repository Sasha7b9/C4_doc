The game is now called Cultivate. It's supposed to be a relaxing game without any real goal other than to grow a nice garden. I have made a video showing basic features of the game. Any feedback is appreciated!
Watch on YouTube


*Original post*
Introduction
Hello! I've been working on a game for a little over two months now, and I figured it was time for a post. I am a solo indie developer, working on a game called Magical Plants. It's planned for PC/Mac. The goal of the game is to grow and sell plants, and use plants to kill monsters in combat. I guess you could say it's a tycoon/tower-defense/rpg-hybrid. Plants can have parts imbued with magic, so plants can cast spells such as fire balls, spray water, healing etc.

Any feedback is appreciated!

Everything is work in progress!

C4 2011-03-14 16-25-37-57.png

C4 2011-03-14 16-21-20-46.png


A rough feature list.
  • Grow living plants
    Choose the best suited pot and soil for your seed and make sure it gets enough water, warmth, nutrition and light to see it grow into a beautiful plant.
  • Endless variations of plants
    Combine DNA to grow plants of all forms and colors. Can you crossbreed the ultimate killer plant? Or one so beautiful no one can resist it?
  • Strategic plant growing
    Place fire plants to give other plants warmth and light, or watering plants to keep them moist. Place your most valued plants where enemies will have a hard time reaching them, and surround them with combative plants to defend them from insects, thieves and other intruders.
  • Story and missions
    Help the king rid the land of evil monsters, and find out where they are coming from. Help townsfolk with their tasks.
  • Fluctuating market
    Go to the market place to sell your plants. Rare plants will earn you the most money, and keeping them beautiful and healthy is important to be able to sell them and get happy customers.
  • Strategic defensive combat
    Take you plants to farms, caves, cellars and more to get rid of those evil invaders. The right type of plant at the right place is crucial to success.
  • Train skills in growing, business and combat
    Train skills to focus on growing plants in large quantities or of great quality. Improve your salesman skills and charisma to sell more plants, and train leadership and tactics to crush the enemy forces.
  • Upgrade buildings
    Upgrade your greenhouse to keep intruders out or to get more space. Buy a larger and better market stand, or put up a large sign to attract customers.

I have posted some updates to my personal blog: http://pontuseriksson.se

The idea has changed more towards building your own garden (you get a small patch of soil). At the end of each day you will get paid depending of what people (AI) thought of it. You will still be able to sell plants. At the moment combat is more or less scrapped.

Decorations can be placed to increase attraction, and some decorations/plants will attract certain types of insects. Beautiful insects will increase visitors' attraction, and some insects will be pollinators. I will probably also include fairies that may move into your garden and affect it in some way (boost growth etc.), if you have a fairy house.

Online stuff that would be nice to have is share-your-garden. You upload your garden, and then people can download it and visit (not change anything), and rate it. This could also mean you can have competitions where players are supposed to build a certain type of garden and upload.
