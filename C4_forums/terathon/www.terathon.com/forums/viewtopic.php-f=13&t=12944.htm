Still the basic problem of pathfinding remains and is always the same. No easy and out of the box cheap solution. I dunno. I used a network of nodes in the past, the classical A* approach. While the A* algorithm to search a graph isn't a problem, creating the navigation mesh is and its the biggest problem in my eyes. Not to beat a dead horse here again, there must be some useful way to create a mesh. The recast stuff was nice, but like I said elsewhere when I replaced the test world with the C4 island world it completely broke down. Sooner or later I will have to deal with that since theres no way around it for me, I need it for my game.

What I did in the past was to create a 1 meter grid over the whole world in a converter. Can take a few hours, but thats not a problem. The size of a grid of several Km on the other hand, is one. That's why I want to look into this navigation polygon principle that recast uses. I guess the principle of navigating these polygons should managable, again the creation is the problem. Maybe theres no way around manual creation in the end.
