The 31st Available for Pre-Order

by Eric Lengyel » 17 Mar 2011, 05:04

The game we are developing here at Terathon, called The 31st, is now available for pre-order on the game's website:

http://www.the31stgame.com/

The first episode of this game is going to be released in October 2011, and it can be ordered today for only $10. Two more episodes will follow in 2012, and they will cost less than the first episode.

Not only does pre-ordering provide support that ultimately makes The 31st a better game, but it also helps stimulate faster development of the C4 Engine.

The game's website is currently in the early stages of development. Much more content about the game will be posted in the coming weeks. You can also follow the game on Twitter and YouTube (links are on the game's website).

rauru wrote:As I can see, the game is a more extended version than the demo of the engine but they share the same mechanics, character set, gameplay, and so on.


Please do not assume that the game will be anything like the C4 demo, because they are very different. Yes, the game will have most of the same enemy characters and the same weapons (but with some new ones you haven't seen), but there is a lot more AI and behavior variation in the game, much larger and much more detailed levels, a bunch of power-ups you've never seen, secrets to find, and last but not least, an actual storyline.
