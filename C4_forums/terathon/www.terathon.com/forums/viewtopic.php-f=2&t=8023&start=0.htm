Logo Contest Winner

by Eric Lengyel » 27 Sep 2009, 16:36

Logo #13 by JamesMK has been chosen as the winner of the C4 Engine logo contest. Congratulations!

It was a close race at the end, but we felt that logo #13 provided the best symbol for C4 combined with a clean design that works well in print and on screen. Logo #13 received the most number of votes overall, but that wasn't the only criterion used to choose a winner. The excellent animation made for the logo certainly didn't hurt, but I want to reiterate that the logos were judged on a variety of attributes, and I believe the outcome of the contest would have been the same without it.

We are working with JamesMK to pick the final colors that will be used in the logo. Suggestions for these colors are welcome.

The updated submissions for the finalists can be seen here:

http://www.terathon.com/c4engine/logosfinal2009.php
