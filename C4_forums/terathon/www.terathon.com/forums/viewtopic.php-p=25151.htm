Eric Lengyel wrote:As for the popping, I think it's speaker-dependent. The speakers I've tested on here haven't had a problem, but I'll try out a larger variety.


Yep, the speakers make a big difference.

The distortion I heard in my headphones (I tried both Sony MDR-NC6 and Sennheiser 2270 headphones) was greatly reduced when I played the sound on my powered monitors with a subwoofer. (I was careful to throw a grenade from the same spot, without moving in between tests.)

Perhaps this could be mitigated with a high-pass filter, to attenuate sounds below a certain cutoff frequency, to avoid distortion for sounds that can't be played by smaller speakers? A popup menu option in the Audio Settings for "Headphones", "Desktop Speakers", and "Floor speakers with Subwoofer" would make this setting more accessible for folks who don't understand the underlying concept, but a slider for the cutoff frequency would be nice for advanced users. Perhaps the popup menu options could automatically adjust a "High-pass cutoff frequency" slider that was just underneath the popup menu?
