C4 Engine Logo Contest

by Eric Lengyel » 09 Sep 2009, 19:10

We are holding a week-long contest to create a new C4 Engine logo. The community will vote on the entries to select the winning logo, and the winner will receive a $350.00 cash prize. For details, see the contest page:

http://www.terathon.com/c4engine/contest.php

You do not need to be a C4 Engine licensee to participate. In fact, if you win, you can use the prize to get a free license to the engine.
