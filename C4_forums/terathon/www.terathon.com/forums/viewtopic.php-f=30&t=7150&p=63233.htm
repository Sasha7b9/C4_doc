Meet ToolBox

by commador » 11 Apr 2009, 03:28

Sup everyone,

We (read...mostly Ph03nix ) have create an internal application that works inside C4 to handle alot of mundane things, and special tools we have created to manage and analyze our project. It has a few bells and whistles but is pretty simple overall. It has some bugs (previously selected elements remain red) but its stable enough we decided to lift the veil. Since we are waiting for 1.6 before we move forward on our game project, we decided to spend the time over the past few weeks to create a one stop tool(box). It has a built in chat which automatically logs the user on when they launch the tool. Username and avatar image are controlled from the toolbox dropdown menu. Some elements do not work as of yet however. Anyway, any questions or comments just holler.
