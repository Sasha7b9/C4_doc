Niteen,hi!

*I would strongly suggest that you work through all the demo examples shown in the User's Manual.

*Also keep in mind that most of the examples have to be modified a bit since the engine has come along quite a ways since the book was released.

*Some stuff that comes to my mind :

- You have to add a physics node before you can spawn a player into a level. Otherwise the player character would jog in place but not move at all when you press the keys.

-The terrain example given in the User's Manual is outdated. Follow the one given in the Wiki instead.

*Will update as I find more stuff.
