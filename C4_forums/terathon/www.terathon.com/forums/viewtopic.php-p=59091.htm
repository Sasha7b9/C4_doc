You made a mistake by punting your Nvidia card. Nvidia's OpenGL drivers are extremely stable and their engineers are extremely thorough when it comes to features working correctly. ATI's drivers tell a far different story. I'm sure you have a different perception than I do, but the early problems with Nvidia's drivers under Vista no longer exist. From a programmer's point of view, Nvidia provides a vastly superior experience. I never have to waste time working around bugs in Nvidia's drivers, but I'm forced to spend hours to days working around problems with the ATI drivers every time we make a new release.
