Blender 2.5 Animation

by CodeNewt » 23 Sep 2011, 12:43

So I'm trying to learn to use blender, because I think its good that if I want to code some simple games that I at least get a feeling for and can appreciate the modeling / animation process. Any ways, I've been following a bunch of videos getting a feel for modeling and made a simple character, I named him Bob.

Bob.png


Funny looking burlap guy, isn't he? I kind of went for a Little Big planet stylized character. I found a free burlap texture, which I did the whole UV texturing thing, along with a Normal Map I generated off the burlap texture. Took me almost 2 weeks to learn how to model / uv wrap to just make something this simple. Yeesh. But I am pretty satisfied with the modeled result.

Side Note: I have a whole new level of appreciation for 3D artists at this point, what you guys do and make (and then make it look realistic) is utterly insane.

Right. So I have the model and started rigging him and trying to get a simple "walk" animation to import into C4 and I'm running into a whole BUNCH of problems. Like warping of the mesh, the model only seems to twist a little when the animation is playing, the mesh seems rotated, etc etc. Just a whole bunch of different problems.

I've googled around a bit and I think I read somewhere that the current Blender (2.5) Collada exporter doesn't export IK Constraints properly. (edit: looks like its more of that the exporter doesn't support bone animation, period.)

Basically I just have no idea what the process for exporting from Blender and importing into C4 is. Does anyone have any videos or websites or have any experience they can impart to try to make this process a little bit easier for this not-so-artistic user? I have looked at dozens of videos on Rigging and Animation, and I can get the basic animation to look the way I want IN Blender, just it doesn't want to work in C4. And I think its the Blender Collada Exporter that is messing everything up.

I can't tell you how much I would appreciate even an inkling of information here to help me get past this hurdle...
