David Vasquez wrote:
Eric Lengyel wrote:...a transmission map.


Is that a new image map type? Does build 1.6 include transmission maps in the Material Manager settings?


I don't really have any plans to add more texture slots to the standard settings in the material manager because it complicates the translation to shader graphs, which is the only thing used internally by the engine. The transmission map is really just a generic texture fetch in the light shader.

I felt it was necessary to provide you with different point of view (from one of your customers). I hope I have something terribly wrong, but this is my impression of what's going on in recent months/years.
