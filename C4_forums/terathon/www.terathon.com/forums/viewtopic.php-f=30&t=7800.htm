Re: Graphics Test Room

by Eric Lengyel » 10 Aug 2009, 17:00

Razor wrote:2. I have a little problem: When I was in the shader editor and changed something in it, I cant use most of the material properties anymore. I hope you know the problem and tell me, what I am making wrong.


Once a material is converted to a shader graph, you can't change the standard attributes any more because they don't necessarily have any meaning in your shader. Once you start editing the shader graph, you have to keep making changes there -- you've moved from simple materials to advanced materials, and you can't go back.

Razor wrote:3. I have a bit experience with converting, importing and setting up a hole scene in C4, but sometimes I have a problem with flickering lights, objects or reflections. What can I do to solve the problem?


Can you elaborate, or post a test world (in a ZIP file)?

Razor wrote:4. Maybe you can see in the screenshots, that on the walls are some light circles. Can I improve the blending of these circles?


That's called Mach Banding, and it's due to the slowly varying intensity in the attenuation texture. The only thing you can really do about it is to put a little bit of texture on the wall to hide it.
