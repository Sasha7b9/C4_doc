by TheRhinoDude » 10 Jul 2010, 21:31

mwituni wrote:
Eric Lengyel wrote:Yes, if an impostor node is detached from the scene, it causes a vertex buffer rebuild.

Do you have any plans to make any changes regarding this?
You mentioned long ago you are aiming to support large stream-able terrains. I'm not sure this will stream that well.
Or is the idea to load them in as blocks of zones or something? And if so - will the buffer be ok as they are loading / unloading?


The vertex buffer will have to be rebuilt anyway when new terrain chunks are paged in and out, with the imposters along with them.

Eric never said that adding/removing imposters didn't work. It's just not performant...so don't do it every frame, or even every few frames. But doing it as part of a wholesale page in/out is expected and unavoidable, because it will be necessary anyway given how C4 handles terrain..

Look at it this way: the vertex buffer rebuild is trivial compared to the overhead for loading a paged terrain block. A complete mechanism has to be engineered so that this happenes in the background anyway. I have to think that the vertex buffer rebuild time is insignificcant compared to loading the terrain block in the background. It's much more of an engineering problem to make that work seemlessly with no interruption of frame rate than the overhead of rebuilding the index buffer. Once he solves that, the vertex buffer rebuild is a non-issue.

Plus, you shouldn't see blocks streaming in and out every few frames. It should be more like every few seconds at most, to more realistically, every tens of seconds to minutes.

Personally I'm dying for this feature (terrain paging / background loading and unloading of terrain blocks). Both C4 projects I'm working on could use this technology. Once we have that plus ground foliage and the new water effects, C4 will have one of the absolute best solutions for sacrifice-free massive outdoor terrains of any engine at any price, and definately the best for any engine with source for < $50k.

- Ryan
