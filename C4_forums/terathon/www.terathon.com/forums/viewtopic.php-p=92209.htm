C4 Engine now 50% for all former Torque users

by Eric Lengyel » 11 Nov 2010, 17:47

In response to the announcement that the makers of the Torque engine product line are going out of business, Terathon Software is currently offering a 50% discount on C4 Engine licenses for any previous owner of any Torque engine license for a limited time. To take advantage of this discount, simply use the discount code TORQUE on the C4 Engine order page, and then send a copy of your original Torque license email to sales@terathon.com, with any sensitive information removed, so we can verify your eligibility.

TheRhinoDude wrote:- When you say "any Torque license", do you mean any engine sold my Garage Games, period? Including mobile and XNA, and including "binary-only" licensees?


We mean a license for any product with "Torque" in the name, including binary-only licenses.

TheRhinoDude wrote:- Are binary-only GG licenees going to be able to get the 50% discount on C4 Standard?


Yes, no matter what Torque product you had before, you can get 50% off any C4 license.

TheRhinoDude wrote:- If somebody can prove they have paid for N of these engine products, can he get the C4 discount for N different C4 licenses (ie to be given as gifts or payment to team members for example)? Or is this a limit 1 per customer deal?


A former Torque user can currently purchase any number of C4 seats at 50% off regardless of how many seats they had with Torque.

TheRhinoDude wrote:- Are you going to require that the GG licensees were purchased prior to your offer, since many $99 GG licenses are still on sale?


Yes, to take advantage of our offer, the Torque product must have been purchased on or before Nov 10, 2010.

TheRhinoDude wrote:There are a lot of products that were licensable from there over the years, here's a short (surely incomplete) list, not counting different versions:
