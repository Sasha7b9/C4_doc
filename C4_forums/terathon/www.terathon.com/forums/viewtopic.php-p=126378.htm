Re: Version 4.0 Final Now Available

by Jesse Meyer » 15 Oct 2014, 23:33

ska wrote:Thanks very much Jesse.

So this marker will be the place that the camera will be during the shader compilation, when the level start? And how do I know where should I put this marker?
The idea it´s to let the warming up process faster?


AFAIK you can place as many markers as you want, but the cost is increased loading time (at the benefit of reduced ingame stuttering) since more scenes are being rendered, thereby increasing the amount of shaders being pre-generated.

I imagine the best places to have them located are areas with lots of unique materials, or places where it's likely that additional shader combinations are needed.
