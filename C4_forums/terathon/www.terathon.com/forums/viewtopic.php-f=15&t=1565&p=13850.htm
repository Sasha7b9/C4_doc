by Eric Lengyel » 19 Dec 2006, 00:59

The opacity texture is ignored if you're not rendering refraction. Transparency is always grabbed from the alpha channel of the diffuse texture. For your geometry to show transparency, you'll want to select it in the editor, hit Ctrl-I for the Get Info dialog, go to the Rendering tab, and check "Render geometry in effect pass". (You'll also want to turn off shadows in the same dialog.)
