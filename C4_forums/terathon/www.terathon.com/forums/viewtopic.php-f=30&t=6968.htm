C4 Games My Students Made.

by gogo » 21 Mar 2009, 21:27

Hi all,

I finished teaching my course on Technical Game Dev. I, a third-year course, here at WPI, and thought I'd share a link to the final projects the students did with C4. These were done in conjunction with the sister course, Artistic Game Dev. I, and each game was done by a team of at least two tech people from my course coupled with at least two art people from the other course. The games (tech/art demos really) went from concept to completion in 6 weeks (!), and so should be "judged" accordingly.

Downloadable and readme files (PLEASE read these!) for most of the games are accessible here: http://web.cs.wpi.edu/~gogo/courses/imgd3000_2009c/projects/final/.

Links to the final projects from previous years are at the bottom of the page.

The Guide was a HUGE help this year!!

Comments?

-Rob
