Re: Need a Mac ... which one

by Rastar » 30 Dec 2014, 12:20

I am using a late 2011 15" Macbook Pro (2.4 GHz i7, Intel HD3000 / Radeon HD6770M, 8GB). C4 is building and running fine on that one, Hell's Mouth gets around 30 +-10 FPS @1400x900, all graphics settings on high. The only thing that is a little annoying is that the fan is kicking in pretty quickly, and it isn't a quiet one. I put an SSD disk in, that helped a lot in terms of general responsiveness.

If you actually want to build and run on that machine a lot, I think I would refrain from the Air and rather head for the Pro. I really love to work on the Macbook (it's my machine at work, at home I am running a Windows PC). Things just work, and having a "real" Linux-like terminal is just great.
