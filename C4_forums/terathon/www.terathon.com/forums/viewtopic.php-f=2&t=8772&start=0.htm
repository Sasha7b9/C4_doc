by Eric Lengyel » 19 Feb 2010, 19:01

If you are attending GDC next month, you'll be able to find me promoting my new book, Game Engine Gems, Volume One, at the Jones and Bartlett booth on Thursday and Friday, March 11-12. There will also be a book signing at the IGDA booth on Friday, March 12 between 2:00 and 3:00pm. I will also be giving away new C4 Engine T-shirts and posters. I hope to meet some more C4 Engine users at this event.
