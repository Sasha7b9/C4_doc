by Adam Golden » 29 Sep 2010, 15:39

Thanks guys

razer wrote:Great! How big is terrain?

This map is 2km square, but it wraps in every direction, so a course can continue almost indefinitely (up to floating point limitations - I have most of the code in place to work around that but haven't finished it). Objects can be placed in the worlds at a relative or absolute position - meaning they either repeat with the map, or only appear during a certain "iteration" of it - and can also be assigned an "after checkpoint" and/or "before checkpoint" value which is checked on visibility determination.. very useful for this gameplay mode (although not all modes will have checkpoints). The foliage changes every time you play, as it's positioned procedurally based on blend textures, but everything else is placed and is always the same.

by Adam Golden » 29 Sep 2010, 19:12

Thanks for all the positive feedback everyone.

Requirements are keyboard and mouse (touchpads just won't do considering the accuracy you'll need), graphics cards you likely have since you're here, and a dual core processor is strongly recommended. If you have a single core, there's a fallback in place which automatically reduces foliage and hopefully that will help keep frame rates high for those players - I haven't experimented much with optimizing for that, given it was a system requirement until recently.

The graphics options default to 1024x640, 2x multisample, glow and distortion. You're free to modify this in variables.cfg of course (in C:\Users\<user>\AppData\Roaming\Wingball\ on Windows 7) to fit your system better. I get a consistent 80-100fps with a quad 2.4ghz and 8800 GTS. Marty (Kamikazi UK) was reporting about 40fps on his laptop, but I'm not sure of his specs. I've tested gameplay down to 24fps and it's still playable, but I wouldn't recommend less than 30 because of how fast you're moving and the limited time to react.
