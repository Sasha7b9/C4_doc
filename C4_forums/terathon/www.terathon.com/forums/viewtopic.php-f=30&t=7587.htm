Re: Another Foliage Thread

by Eric Lengyel » 20 Jun 2009, 16:03

MachineryFrank wrote:The only problem is: Not all polygons cast shadows in C4, depending on their orientation towards the light. They render 2-sided but do not cast shadows 2-sided.


In version 1.5.9, there is a separate flag for two-sided shadow mapping that is set in code. This has been changed in version 1.6, and the shadowing works as expected for two-sided geometry. I am also including some shader functionality that makes shading of two-sided surfaces easier -- you can do it in 1.5.9, but it's not easy.
