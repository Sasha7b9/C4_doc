VS 2012

by a2k » 23 Apr 2013, 13:01

Finally made the transition a few days ago, and I'm enjoying VS2012 over VS2010. It really does feel like 2010 was a trainwreck, and it makes me cringe when I have to use it at work, due to the broken intellisense, go-to-declarations, etc. Not sure how much of it was aesthetic related updates to match with Win8, but otherwise, I think it's a better IDE, and would highly recommend that people update their projects to this. Of the few features I've noticed so far, the Solution Explorer has finally grown up and lists methods, members, enums, etc in outline view underneath each file. How long has Eclipse (and other IDEs possibly) had this?
