Re: Blender To C4

by Oleg » 22 Apr 2010, 11:11

There are tree objects in the scene. Lower one has no texture assigned to it.
Two other have textures, but "Map Input" is set to "Orco". Select both objects, press F5 to bring up material buttons, in the "Map Input" tab press "UV" button.

And the most important thing - there is no UV mapping in all three objects. C4 requires UV mapping even if the object has no texture.
