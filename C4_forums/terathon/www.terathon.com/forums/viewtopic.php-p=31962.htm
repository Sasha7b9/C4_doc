Build 145 Beta Now Available

by Eric Lengyel » 21 Nov 2007, 03:03

The beta release of build 145 is now available at the following address:

https://www.terathon.com/c4engine/beta/index.php

Only authorized accounts have access to the beta release. If you have a good reason to access the beta release, send me a PM explaining that reason, and be sure to include your license number, which starts with "C4T". Requests without license numbers will be ignored. Do not send your download key. You will not receive an acknowledgment of beta access, so you'll need to just check your account to see if you got approved.

Valid reasons for accessing the beta release include things like needing to test a bug fix that affects your product or having multiple GPU or OS configurations to test on.
