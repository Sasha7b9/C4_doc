Re: Character image of the day

by Eric Lengyel » 23 Jun 2009, 16:53

RonHiler wrote:Just out of curiosity, what distances are you using for your LOD cutoff points?


I don't know the exact distances off the top of my head (they depend on FOV and viewport size, so I'd have to do some calculations), but the switch from LOD 0 to LOD 1 happens around 5-6 meters away from the camera.

RonHiler wrote:Also, is there any advantage to turning off other aspects of the models via LOD? For instance, normal maps would be useless beyond an LOD level of 0 (or maybe 1), but does not rendering them give any speedup?


This particular model has a lot of information in the normal map, so it would be necessary to keep it on all LODs. However, things like parallax mapping and horizon mapping can be phased out with distance to get a faster shader. The benefit isn't all that big, though, because shaders have a per-pixel cost, and the models cover far fewer pixels when they're further away.
