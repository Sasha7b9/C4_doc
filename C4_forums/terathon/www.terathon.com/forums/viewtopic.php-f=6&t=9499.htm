Re: How to get reverb to work?

by Eric Lengyel » 23 Jul 2010, 23:29

To duplicate what I did in the Lasers world, do this:

1) Open Tutorial/worlds/Lasers.wld.
2) Go to the top view and make the right zone the current target zone.
3) Open the Spaces page (if it's not already open) and draw an acoustics space the roughly covers the right zone.
4) In a side view, adjust the height of the acoustics space to approximately the height of the zone.
5) Go to the Node Info window for the acoustics space and increase the reverb decay time to 2.0 or so.
6) Open the Sources page and drag out an omni source in the top view so that the sphere is a little bigger than the zone.
7) In the size view, move the source up so it's not in the floor.
8) Go to the Node Info window for the source and select a sound resource.
9) Play the level.

You should hear your sound looping with reverb. The sounds played for the panels will also have reverb.
