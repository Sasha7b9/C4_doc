Re: Help with Animation Import

by Twisted Helix » 12 Jul 2011, 07:26

Make sure the animation is Z up and not Y up, although in my experience Y up animations tend to explode rather than twist oddly.

If its come from MAX then its likely Z up (default), however if its come from Maya it will be Y up (default).

Anchor X-Y is for animations where you have root motion and you want to remove it, so you can take a normal walk animation that moves in model space, and then just anchor it to zero ... very handy. Anchor Z will be something similar for jump animations when you want to apply the vertical offset in the game rather than have it as root motion in the animation.
