Re: Blender To C4

by Jack » 09 Aug 2012, 09:17

Here are the steps I did:

1. Select the gun and press ALT+P choose "Clear and Keep Transformation".
2. Gun still selected and the Armature of the soldier needs to be in Pose Mode. Shift select bone "Bip01 R Hand" and press STRG+P choose "Bone".
3. Remove the Armature modifier and all vertex groups of the gun.
4. Select MuzzleFlash geometry and press ALT+P choose "Clear and Keep Transformation".
5. Remove the Armature modifier from MuzzleFlash geometry.
6. Select MuzzleFlash geometry and shift select the gun then press STRG+P and choose "Object".
http://www.mk-lab.de
