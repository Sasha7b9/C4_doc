Re: Problems with import from 3DMax 2010 (crash)

by caldoer » 14 Apr 2011, 05:17

These files load into my 2.5.2 PC version of C4 without crashing or error, but the skeleton remains in a centered position while the base mesh seems to start off leaning off to one side. If I import the files into Maya2011 I get a warning about the RootNode not being part of the Bind Pose, and there being no initial binding position for the skin so it creates its own guess about what it should be. Depending on which plugin I use the "guess" pose varies from exactly to what shows up in C4 to something closer, IE through the middle of the head through the heads center and the fingers in the fingers, but not perfect. Either way I have a skeleton that moves that I still need to attach to the skin before it animates, C4 at least does this automatically for me.

I don't know what might be causing C4 to crash on you, but checking out the bind position for your rig setup sounds like it might be a good place to look. Good luck!
