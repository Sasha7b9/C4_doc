I decided a while ago that I was going to try to do more detailed weapon models for the first-person view, including higher-detail hands, and then set things up to where only the first-person model is drawn in first-person view, and the third-person model is drawn everywhere else. Re: First-person gun attachment point inquiry

by Eric Lengyel » 01 Mar 2013, 00:38

In the demo game, the camera is always positioned relative to the player's model node, and the weapon is attached to a "mount node" that is a subnode of the model node. The positions are constant, so they always have the same relative position with each other. (We're going to be expanding this so that the camera and weapon move around a little. Right now, there is some animation on some of the weapons, but that's all.)

Re: First-person weapon attachment point inquiry

by Eric Lengyel » 01 Mar 2013, 01:43

There's really no standard or preferred way to do this. You're free to attach cameras and weapons to a model however you want. For the camera, you don't even have to attach it -- you could just keep setting the transform to be the model's transform plus some offset. It's completely up to you.
