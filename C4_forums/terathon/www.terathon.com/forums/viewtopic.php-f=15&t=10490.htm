Re: How to make worlds..

by Javier Moya » 10 Mar 2011, 14:20

You can use the C4 WE to make levels. Hell's mouth demo map was made in a month and a lot of geometry was made within the WE (and I'm not just refering to the terrain). Walls, ceilings and floors are quite obvious, but all the light objects (including the big poles) were made inside the editor too, as well as fences, the helipad, the workbenck, cables, big tanks, generators, pipes and their support structures, traps ... There are several objects made outside (3ds max), because I decided to do it so, but they could have been done within the editor with no problem. Obviously, there are objects which cannot be done, as the big skull, but you can do a lot of things with the editor at its current sate.

Maybe this article is useful for you (look for the PDF):

http://udn.epicgames.com/Three/ModularLevelDesign.html

Hope it helps.
