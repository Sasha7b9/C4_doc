Re: Creating Terrain

by Eric Lengyel » 10 Oct 2014, 19:28

You can't import a 4096x4096 terrain because it takes up too much memory. You'll need to reduce this to 1024x1024 and then import after dragging out a 4096x4096 box. (Just set your grid size to 1024 and drag out 4 squares, or something similar.)

by Eric Lengyel » 13 Oct 2014, 17:31

OK, that's waaay too small of a square for such a large terrain image. You're causing the voxel size to be extremely tiny, but your height is stretched out over 100 meters, so there's a huge number of voxels being created vertically. This is why it's slow. Change the grid settings and drag out a much larger square instead. (The terrain tutorial level uses a grid spacing of 10, and you'd normally drag out over 3-4 major gridlines there, meaning that the terrain is at least 300x300 m.)

Re: Creating Terrain

by Eric Lengyel » 13 Oct 2014, 18:18

GameWizard wrote:Is it that the total number of voxels created is the image size hxw multiplied by the total height ( the 100+32+32)??


No. The width and height of your imported image divided into the actual size of the terrain block that you've dragged out determines the voxel size. If you drag out a 4x4 m block and import a 1024x1024 image, then the size of each voxel is 4/1024 = 0.00390625 m. Voxels are cubic, so if you specify a height of 100 m, then 100 / 0.00390625 = 25600 voxels are needed to cover the full height of your terrain, and the 32 extra voxels are added to the top and bottom of that. That's too large.

GameWizard wrote:What if a world has multiple blocks??


They are completely independent.

GameWizard wrote:What about the fact that the 8 bit raw built vertically?


Did you drag out the terrain block in a side view?
