The more I read psychological studies on marketing the more I think we're only getting slightly better at understanding the results of our attempts predicting the rate of change of an irregularly shifting target. Optimizing for such a low level system like our psychological makeup to me, only makes pragmatic sense when all the higher level systems are operating well (high quality product, demand in the economy, positive reputation with the consumer, proper value evaluation, etc).

I am not sure this crap works too well on programmers. I was watching some show pointing out psychological tricks and none of them worked on me.

I think number 3 is just common sense. Of course you need to enumerate just what you are getting to justify your price, and split things up to help people gain interest. You can do that with any product too.

1. Standard. Get the current version of C4 AND the world editor $150. You get the integrated sound and video tools FOR FREE!!!
2. C4 advanced. Get the above AND the C4 Advanced Physics engine including detailed cloth and ocean simulation. Just $50 extra!
3. C4 advanced plus. Get all of the above plus A YEAR OF UPDATES! Just $300! Add additional years for just $50 each at time of initial purchase only! After this price may go up SO ACT NOW.
4. C4 Gold. Get ALL of the above AND a LIFETIME OF UPDATES for the INCREDIBLY LOW PRICE OF $750!!!

I am sure you could split up C4 into a whole number of separate points without stretching the reality by adding in BS points.

At any rate at this point obviously the graphics are amazing. C4 mostly needs more tools, more platforms, more ease of use and lots and lots of work at finding ways to monetize. Things like the particle editor and fullscreen post-processing are a huge step in the right direction. Though honestly I think post-processing is usually a "lazy man" approach to try to make things look good or fit a certain style. Usually some horrible crap style.
