by mikec » 07 Jul 2006, 13:38

Believe it or not, this debate has actually raged on for a long time in the prorammer community and still does. I've been on many teams and have worked with people of both camps. The current team I am on follows the 2 hour rule. You first try to figure things out on your own for a couple of hours, and if you can't do that, then you seek out others. The reason for this is that with the couple of hours experience, you'll also be more focused on what your difficulty actually is, and can also explain what steps you've already tried. The argument against asking someone right away is that you are stopping the productivity of someone else, and some of the time it is needless. Don't take me wrong, I'm not taking sides, but it is an interesting topic of discussion. This type of thing is exactly what prompted Microsoft with starting knowledge bases. Of course, it is silly to suffer on a problem for days and not seek help, at that point I agree with flashjackson in that it always makes good sense to just ask for help. But this topic is digressing...

The simple answer to the original question is that every format to be supported by C4 requires an importer, and the only officially supported importer right now is the Collada one.
