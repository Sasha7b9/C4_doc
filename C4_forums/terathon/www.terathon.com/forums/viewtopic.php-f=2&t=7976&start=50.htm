So here are my 2 cents to the logo discussion.

A logo has to fit many options beside looking good, professional and being recognizable.

It has to be readable even if it is very small. Not only for on business cards and documents. But for a game engine also on screenshots, folder icons etc.

Furthermore it as to be reproducable even in just black and white for watermarks on screenshots, sending documents via fax or for stamps.

It has also to meet the style of the product etc it stands for. In this case the engine itself and not the games that are made with it.

To meet this requirements often less is more in the design decision for a good logo. It should make use of just a few basic shapes that are ordered clever enough to make people look at it and remind it. This simple shapes can be shown in black and white as well as with some effects on it or in 3D.

I think the C4 Engine needs a logo that brings out it's professionality, it's clear structure, it's power and it's progressiveness.


Some of the already locked as favourites:

02 - Very harmonious. Simple and effective. Would be a good candidate.

07 - I love the design But I'm afraid it is too playful for a engine like C4.

13 - I really like the last two variations of this concept. Also a good candidate.

25 - Very effectful. But the grunge design is way too much for a good logo. It would fit more a game like "Gears of War" than a engine. I'd prefer a clear font on this because looking this logo is very hard to read in a small scale.

38 - Really love the colors. Some tweaks on the swoosh and another font instead of the spiderman font.

53 - I like the red color. This one would be great to animate for a splash screen. Also good readable in a small scale.



Logos that look good as well:

44 - My favourite of the not already locked logos. This should be definitive in the favourites list.

51 - I like the idea a lot. Especially the clean ohne without the gradients. Only thing that bugs me is the hole in the middle.


Overall feeling: There are some great designs in the contest and I wish good luck to all of you.
