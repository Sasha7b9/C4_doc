Sounds and more.

by David Vasquez » 05 Aug 2007, 00:45

The sound static seems to come and go depending on where the player is standing.

For example, I didn't hear any static for the grenade from within the fan room, but when I threw one into the fan room from outside the ground-level hallway, there was very bad distortion in the explosion sound.

On a more positive note, A/B'ing with build 136, the reverb makes the grenade bouncing sounds more realistic and menacing. I noticed that there is even some doppler-effect frequency shifting when the grenade bounces from left to right, or when I run after one.

And thanks for all the feature requests that made it into build 137, especially moving the various settings windows out of the engine, and the greater control over sound volume, which will be very useful for panel sound effects. Good stuff!
