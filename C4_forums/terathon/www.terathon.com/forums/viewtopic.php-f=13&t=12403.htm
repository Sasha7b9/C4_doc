by Jesse Meyer » 28 Nov 2012, 02:21

MACK wrote:The beta seems to have an absolute requirement that Photoshop be installed.
It looks sort of cool but also I am not really sure of its "niche".
Is it a model viewer with Photoshop Material preset tweaker?

Also, I did not see any list of model formats that it will handle. So, do you happen to know what model format "dDo" uses for applying the materials?


Its general purpose is to provide a base for working on textures. dDo will analyze your textures and can apply a number of different effects to them (grime, dust) in clever ways that you often can control via slope, object space normals, different weight and distributions, and so forth all within Photoshop. The idea is to keep the artist within a toolset that they are most familiar with. Most of the time artists have to swap between many different applications to complete a texture and this, along with nDo seem to reduce that need quite a bit.

I think .obj is what it supports officially although Davaris may be correct.
