Re: C4 Engine Logo Contest Entries

by a2k » 17 Sep 2009, 23:06

Here's the criteria that my eye is looking at:

C4 is bold and strong.
The C and 4 take up an equal amount of space.
C4 is easily discernible,
The C and 4 are each distinguishable on their own
The logo has style or a clever twist.

So these are my picks, based on that, positives and negatives:

01 - is bold, strong, equal in space, slightly not too distinguishable. May not have much style, but it definitely continues the existing brand recognition from the current logo.

02 - bold, strong, equal, distinguishable and clever. The 4 has all sharp edges while the C retains some rounding, yet each one takes up the same amount of space.

16 - equal, distinguishable, conseratively stylized. The font is not too strong, but the image is a good icon nonetheless. Not sure if image would stand by itself without the text though.

17 - Fits all my criteria, except maybe the style a little.

27 - I like the style and balance. It's just the thin font can hurt it when seen on a T-shirt from far away. It's another take on the number 4 that nobody else has taken, but it runs the risk of looking like C5. The font on Engine seems perfect though.

36 - I think is absolutely the most clever of them, combining the C, 4 AND E. It's very strong, but the C gets lost as "dressing", and almost forms the Fantastic Four symbol. Others have mentioned the Nintendo logo as well.

I apologize if I felt that "looks like" is slamming. I just figure that if it "looks like" something else, then the logo doesn't have an identity of its own.

If any of my comments also seem negative, I'll gladly edit my post too.
