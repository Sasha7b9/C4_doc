Re: Prototyping a MergeAnimator

by Benjamin Dobell » 20 Aug 2012, 22:30

MergeAnimators are useless without two or more sub-animators. I don't know if that's the cause of your crash as I've never tried to use a MergeAnimator without at least two sub-animators, but it's a possibility.

For a MergeAnimator to be useful you'd need something like

Code: Select all
- MergeAnimator
-- BlendAnimator
--- FrameAnimator
--- Frameanimator
-- Any Animator
--- ... Sub-animators if the parent animator is not a FrameAnimator.

In this setup, the merge animator will always cause the result of the second blend animator to be applied to the model, and the first one will be overwritten. I thought you were applying different animations to different parts of the skeleton. The merge animator is not useful unless its inputs target different subtrees of the model being animated.
