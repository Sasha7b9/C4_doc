Re: Windows vs boards

by Eric Lengyel » 19 Feb 2011, 05:30

Boards are kind of a light-weight window or generic container for an interface. They can have interactive widgets in them, but they'll never be considered when the automatic input management determines what input mode to be in. They can't have a frame, close box, resize handle, or anything like that, they can't be dragged around, and they can't appear in the strip. I personally use boards mostly for displaying things in the HUD.

Re: Windows vs boards

by Eric Lengyel » 21 Feb 2011, 15:38

OK, then why is what's happening in the viewport widget happening in the viewport widget? I'm just trying to get the big picture.

The viewport widget must use its own camera to render the world. You don't want to create another camera. Grab the camera from the viewport and assign it to the world. The SetCameraTransform() function is provided for convenience in case you want to use a spherical-coordinate transform for your camera, and it does not need to be used if you don't want to. You can just set whatever transform you want right on the camera node.
