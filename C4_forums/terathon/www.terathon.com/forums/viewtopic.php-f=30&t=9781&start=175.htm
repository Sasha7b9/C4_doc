Re: Wingball 0.5.3 Released

by Adam Golden » 19 Oct 2010, 20:43

wtblife wrote:It actually seems like the path is skinnier in some areas, or maybe it's just not polished completely.

It's intentionally not the same width for the whole course. These levels are still roughs though. I won't be polishing much until the last few months before final release - the main thing for me is to get all functionality and gameplay together and working smoothly asap, and then I'll focus more on design and improvements.

wtblife wrote:Btw are there any plans for multiplayer? That would be too much fun... Also when are these prizes happening and based off what scores?

From the Features page of the website:
"Multi-player gameplay modes are a possibility given significant interest, but are not an immediate concern."

There are significant obstacles to adding multi-player modes to this game. Foremost is that a dedicated server to host that side of things would cost a substantial amount of money (more and more as popularity grows), but this game is free. Another problem is bullet time - how would this affect the other players? Would everyone get slowed down? Or would that be disabled in multi-player sessions? If it wasn't available, it might work, considering that you can beat the courses without it. I think the most reasonable multi-player modes to add would be "ghost" racing against other players. That way everyone could be using bullet time as they wanted, you'd just see them slowing down, rather than having to deal with "time-space" issues. Everyone would start at the same time from a lobby where they could chat etc., and the winners would be determined by the time it took for everyone to complete it. Regardless, if I do add live multi-player modes, it will be after the game is complete, and given the cost of maintaining it, probably only available to people with a Plus account. Leaderboards will always be free, as they have no significant cost - I could have 1000 people at a time playing day and night, and it wouldn't cost a penny more than I already pay for hosting.

Prizes are most likely (initially) to be free Wingball Plus account upgrades, although if that side of this turns into a good business by some stroke of luck, prizes could be tangible (as I have no aversion to giving 1/2 the income beyond the expenses of maintaining the servers back to the players, whether that means cash, games, consoles, or whatever). I won't even bother making a Wingball Plus if there isn't enough interest by the time this game is finished. In that case I would start on something else. Otherwise, Wingball Plus would be the continuation of this game - maybe a new level every month, some new vehicle skins, additional gameplay modes, whatever (haven't thought about it much yet, but that's the general idea). How would you win prizes? That could be determined a lot of ways... maybe the people who played the most number of times that day? Maybe the person who moved up the most number of ranks that day? I'll give it some thought before the game is done - it is definitely part of the plan.

Edit: I guess winners of a multi-player checkpoint race (or other mode) would be based on score, not just time as I mentioned above - as the scores are an amalgam of time, item collection, strategy etc.
