WASAPI and OpenAL

by MrTesla » 14 Sep 2007, 22:19

There's an interesting article in the recent edition of PCGamer magazine, regarding how Microsoft dropped DirectSound from Vista and replaced it with a new API called WASAPI (Windows Audio Session API). Unfortunately this change has caused lots of headaches for people running sound cards like Creative's X-Fi. Specifically you can't get EAX effects to work because Vista forces everything to be processed in software. Yuck.

But thankfully Creative is supporting a free cross-platform audio tool called OpenAL. OpenAL supports 3D-positional audio and EAX effects.

Just thought I'd toss that out for everyone. Looks like there is a great audio system going in C4 now, but OpenAL sounds like a nice backup.
