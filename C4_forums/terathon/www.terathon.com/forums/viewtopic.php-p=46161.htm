Build 149 Final Now Available

by Eric Lengyel » 08 Jul 2008, 17:40

Build 149 is now available for licensed users and can be downloaded from the usual place:

https://www.terathon.com/c4engine/reg/index.php

Some miscellaneous notes:

  • To keep the size of the Data.zip down, the user-contributed demo levels are not included in the licensed download. They will be available through the public demo download.
  • The next release will be called "Version 1.5".
  • The price for a standard license will increase on August 1, 2008, but this does not affect existing licensees or anyone who obtains a license before that date.

Update: The $200 price for a standard license has been extended one week to August 8, 2008.
