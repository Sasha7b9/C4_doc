by datalol-jack » 05 Jan 2015, 14:59

Hi Sabino,

For reference, I ran an older version of C4 on a standard Lenovo Y580 laptop with Nvidia GTX660M (750/750ti equivalent). It ran in the region of 24-68+ FPS, depending on scene and quality settings. That's at 1080p. For best results I had to kill AA and motion blur at times, and the demos remained playable throughout. The editor and my simple apps ran fine.

Now I'm running version 4.1 on a system with an i7-4790, 16GB of RAM, and a slightly clocked GTX 970. Staying at 1080p, with everything cranked up and 4AA/8AF levels, I get 44-207 FPS in the updated demos that come with the engine.

Looking at websites like GPU BOSS shows that your original config would be on par with my laptop spec, if not better. For simple games it should be enough. But for editing and future proofing your rig, it may be worthwhile to go one level up on the GPU front.

Hope this helps.
