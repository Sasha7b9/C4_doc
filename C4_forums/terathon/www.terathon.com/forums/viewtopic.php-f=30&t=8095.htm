by Eric Lengyel » 15 Nov 2009, 04:28

Oovee.co.uk wrote:Does anyone understand why the shadows do this? - This is a solid object and the light is getting through. It does the same for the main building and the fencing outside at the bottoms.


Is that a shadow map? There is an offset applied when the shadows are rendered in order to prevent "shadow acne". Making your surfaces thicker will help. Also, keep in mind that only front-facing polygons with respect to the light source actually cast shadows.
