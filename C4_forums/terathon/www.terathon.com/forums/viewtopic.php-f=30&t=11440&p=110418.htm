Utility vehicles simulator 2012

by Amo » 04 Jan 2012, 09:56

Hey folks, our 4th project with C4 is out in stores. After one Addon for TML's last C4 bus simulator game City Bus Simulator, back in 2010, Bridge the construction game + addon in early 2011 and now the Utility vehicles simulator. It has already been released in October for the German speaking area. Now the english version is on its way to stores. Currently I have no precise release date for the english version but add it as soon as I know something about. But back to the project itself.
The project was a contract job for German Publisher astragon Software GmbH
The sim gives you the opportunity to drive 3 different vehicles:

MPV
standard MPV for transporting jobs and as transportation vehicle for road barriers



Work platform
is used to repair street lanterns and cut branches of trees



High pressure suction vehicle
used for sewer cleaning works.



Gameplay:
You start with a small budget at your company headquarter and owe only the MPV. You accept jobs via your office. By completing them you earn cash and can invest it into road barriers to do these jobs too and earn even more cash to buy more vehicles and equipment.

Features:

-around 2km² driveable terrain including country roads, a city and several bridges
-3 driveable vehicles
-small business system including invoices, paying for fuel and water and stuff

Technical features:
-self developed complex vehicle dynamics system
-Newton Dynamics for non vehicular physics (vehicular only for crashes and after total loss of ground contact)
-self developed vehicle sound system including layers for engine sounds, tranny sounds, turbo sounds, braking, etc. splitted to onload/offload
-complete dynamic day and night cycle system influencing world (AI traffic turns lights on, lanterns)
-AI traffic reacting on the players actions controlled by self developed behaviour tree solution
-AI people with simple "avoid obstacles" system
-traffic control system featuring traffic lights, traffic signs, speed traps and red light traps

For the sake of statistics:
-vehicle dynamics, traffic system and day and night cycle system are reusable and have already been developed in 2010. Day and night cycle was already in use at Bridge! but set to fixed time.
-4.5 months production time
-2 creators (Jack and Me) + 1 external artist for around 3 months

Featured third party stuff:
-Dexsoft vegetation model packs
-modified car models from sugar3d for the AI traffic
-people models from AXYZdesign
-vehicular sounds by Greg Hill
