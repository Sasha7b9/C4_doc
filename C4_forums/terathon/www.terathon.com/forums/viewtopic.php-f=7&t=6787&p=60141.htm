I have done shell bouncing with PhysX. And I have experimented with sizes. You need only slightly larger collision capsule radius than typical shell (if it is too small shells tend to sink in the ground if fallen on the tip). Player won't be able to notice a difference if shadows are off and shell with larger radius even acts sort of more realistic, not sure why
Actually you could see PhysX round shells in that early VoH video featuring dm-core. And that cylinder on top of the preview screen (of that youtube video) was actually flying shell... i guess nobody noticed that

tjheldna, to do shells you would need to add only ~20 lines of custom code to existing JamesH integration.

EDIT: and I didn't turn on CCD.
