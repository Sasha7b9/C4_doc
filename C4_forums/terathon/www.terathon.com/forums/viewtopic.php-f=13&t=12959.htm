I own both math books and they actually make a few references to your book (they mention it every time they do one) and also said somewhere in the book that they could not come up with a better solution than yours so they included it.

The book is clearly intended for people who have a few difficulties with 3D math and need more explanations on fundamental topics such as vectors and matrices.

In my (personal) opinion, I think both books actually complement each other very well since both books contain a lot of identical topics. Their approach is much less strict and reminds me of supervised exercises held by student tutors at my university. As a student, this style is much simpler.

On the other hand, your book is very precise and well written. You have the nice ability to explain a difficult topic in a simple way, yet still maintain a very high level. I favor this clearly as aspiring computer scientist.
