Re: Eye tracking devkit for developers available

by MACK » 12 Jan 2014, 13:14

Eye Tracking will require certain limitations and changes in the UI that may or may not be something that the end-users will like.

For example the first video espouses how wonderful things will be to see the mini-map.. by pressing some button.
What that really is... its a workaround to temporarily turn off eye-tracking to a certain area.

Basically, eye tracking for head motion will drive people a little "crazy", because the screen will move every time they want to look at some score or some value on the HUD. So, they will have to add-in some button to bypass the tracking or a clever HUD trick every time the player wants to look somewhere on the HUD. I think for MANY uses that require a HUD of some kind, this will be too much for the end-user to get used to.

On the other hand, if there is NO HUD... or a very limited HUD, which is only used sparingly then it might be great.

Also not everyone plays video games by following all targets directly, a great deal of gaming is outside the main focus.

Still, it might be cool... to use the eye tracker just to see what it can do.
