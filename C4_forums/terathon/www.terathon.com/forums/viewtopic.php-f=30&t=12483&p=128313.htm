Re: New Book: C4 Engine 3D Game Editing & Programming

by MACK » 29 May 2014, 10:48

The book has several chapters that discuss shaders as part of the content:
1. Paint Spaces and Shaders
2. Creating and Editing VoxelTerrain (including the shaders involved)
3. The Material Manager Layout
4. "Other Shader Processes" which covers several of the shader processes and gives examples.

However, the content is pretty specific and does not go into shaders in great detail. Additionally, the chapters were made prior to the unification of the ambient and light pass, so that does need to be adjusted when you work on the shaders.

I do recommend the wiki which also has a lot of good examples and anyone new to the C4 Engine should really purchase the Beginner's Guide as well:
http://www.terathon.com/wiki/index.php/The_Beginner%27s_Guide_to_the_C4_Engine

I am not aware of anything that discusses how to extend the shaders in any tutorial... so if you find something please let me know.

Just to make a quick note: Macktek continues to offer a payment for qualifying chapter material as part of our ongoing process to improve the book.
If anyone would like to submit a shader chapter I would be glad to look over the content and see if we can add it to the book as an addendum.
