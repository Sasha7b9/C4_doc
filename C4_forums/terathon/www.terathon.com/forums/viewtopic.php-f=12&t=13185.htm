Re: looking for a programmer

by MACK » 24 Sep 2013, 02:48

I too have encountered the same thing. Originally I thought people would be willing to share in the profit (in our case it was sales, big difference).
But, it turns out, most people are completely against it as it is too much risk.
We were quite fortunate to convince people to take that risk for the book. But we also paid up front too, with 2 options or even a mix depending on volume and writer contributor desires.

Anyway, an unknown, with or without a plan is almost doomed to fail with a "share the profit" idea.
Corporations purposefully reduce profit with expense. So, its important not to use profit as a methodology.
Instead we used sales.

Re: looking for a programmer

by MACK » 24 Sep 2013, 10:55

Right. You really have to be that dedicated and show the effort. Ska and his team worked on their C4 project and promoted very frequently with working mini-videos of in game content. Ska also had the luxury of having a programmer early on before the "gap" where he was without.
Keep in mind the amount of work that was done was considerable so their team was able to showcase their work. Nevertheless, It took quite a long time to get a programmer. But, that is certainly one way to do be successful.

So, It can be done. BUT, its extremely difficult. I know because I spent a lot of time talking to Ska about his dilemma, and we were really not sure if he would find a programmer despite all his hard work. Luckily he did.

You might try looking at Ska's posts in showcase. It may provide some incentive and give you some ideas on what programmers would like to see before they dive into a project too.
