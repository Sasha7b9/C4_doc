Re: Vortex of Hostility is in need for some beta testers

by DeRatizator » 01 Mar 2011, 15:29

Ohh, that orb

So here is the long version of explanation about damages.
Damage in VoH is done based on several factors:
    Damage type;
    Damage amount;
    Armor and body parts "covered" by it (not rendered yet but you might get an idea by looking at icons in inventory screen);
    Body part hit;

Further info is relevant mostly for projectiles:

These can have multiple damage types with different characteristics : stopping, armor-piercing, explosion and some others exposed later;
Damage types and damage initial amounts come from ammo type, weapon and upgrade (currently disabled) combinations.

Damage which reaches player is based on effective weapon range, which is calculated in similar fashion as initial damage.
Simple example would be that pistols will have ~50% of power at 50m or so - one of point of reference for damages was a rule that AMP should have enough power to kill with HS at close range but this power should decrease by distance.

First thing which gets damaged is armor, if particular body part is "covered" by it.
Different damage types may interfere differently with damages passed further. Two armors used in current release are reducing stopping damage by some % I don't recall right now. Armor health gets reduced in process by amount it absorbed.

Remaining damage gets further influenced by hit body part. Damage done by hitting head will be more than 2 times larger than chest. To arms and legs even less than chest. I will not give exact numbers to prevent it turning to another "number game"

Recoil is influenced by movement speed, posture and initial recoil amount which is calculated same way as damage or any other item property for that matter
Recoil is much less if alt fire is used, however it still can't be controlled so great. There will be an upgrade to "iron sight" mode soon which will make it more controllable with alt fire.

Are you using gun with "c" in the name and heavy ammo ? Stock versions are supposed to be spray and pray guns for mid-close combat only.
If you find recoil too much then try using versions with "mg " postfix, 100 round mag is a nice bonus. They shoot slower and have lower recoil in general. Also more powerful rounds add to recoil (HT5/Heavy vs others).
