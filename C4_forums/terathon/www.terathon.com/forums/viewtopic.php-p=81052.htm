Re: Forklift Simulator

by Oovee.co.uk » 07 Jan 2010, 10:28

Amo wrote:Cool. I like the way the background blends with the groundplane. How did you achieve that?


Thanks for the comments. I achieved this quite difficultly actually but managed to get there in the end. It was a case of creating a skybox texture (basic grey paint colour) and then adjusting the material settings for the reflection colour to match the skybox colour (of course I had to select a slightly brighter grey).

It gets darker towards the bottom because this is the diffuse grey kicking in (darker grey) and you bring the 'Normal incidence reflection' down to Zero to achieve it.

So the overall effect is dark grey to light grey gradient with reflections generated by the ambient portal.

MachineryFrank wrote:This is a very good looking scene. I like the amount of details and the realistic textures in the models.


Thank you, we decided to opt for the soft shadow baked effect for this particular game.

chrism wrote:This is really cool. The only thing to add is to be realistic the boxes on the pallet should have something holding them altogether (taping or string) on the second image. Doesn't look like they have this. Probably being a bit pedantic about that...


Thank you, I was considering this but the taping would be really small and I was worried about it on older machine with bad AA making it look messy. It's still to be considered though.
