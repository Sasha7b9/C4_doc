Re: Skin Shader

by Eric Lengyel » 20 Oct 2009, 03:00

Nice.

Your specular isn't showing up because you haven't added it into the lighting output.

There are several optimization opportunities here:

1) Instead of dividing by 2.0, multiply by 0.5.
2) For both front and back lighting, you're multiplying three colors into the texture separately. You can reduce these to one multiply by the precalculated product of the colors.
3) Try to use a multiply-add process where the result of a multiply gets fed into an add process.
4) You can use the linear interpolate process to combine the front and back lighting, using the tangent light z as the value of t. This one process can replace the two multiplies, the add, and the invert.
