Re: First attempt with Level Design

by Boxsmith » 19 Sep 2009, 21:57

If you want to fix the blending on the rust, take the rust's texture and remove the concrete background. In C4, select the planes, press ctrl+i, then set them to render as decals.

They'll blend just fine with whatever surface is under them. Hope I'm not patronizing you here or anything, from that second screenshot it just looks like what you're doing is cutting out part of the concrete texture and placing the rust overtop.
