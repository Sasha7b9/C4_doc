Re: Import animation trouble

by Eric Lengyel » 11 Mar 2014, 13:59

FBX and DAE both have serious problems, and it's very common for things to get screwed up when you convert FBX to DAE. If you have the model loaded in 3DS Max, I recommend trying the OpenGEX format, which should work much better for getting the model and animations into C4 correctly. Otherwise, I'd have to look at your DAE files to see why the scale and rotation are happening.
