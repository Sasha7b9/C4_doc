Re: Distance from camera to object's vertex position in shad

by Eric Lengyel » 26 Dec 2012, 23:35

I've added four new interpolant processes to version 3.0 that will supply the tangent-space or object-space unnormalized view vector or light vector. Sending the view vector into the Magnitude 3D process will give you the exact distance from an interpolated vertex position to the camera position. This will be available in the first 3.0 development release.

Eric Lengyel
