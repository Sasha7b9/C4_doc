I found 1 bug with Kit management which I though would be worth sharing before some of you go insane

Do not try to create a new Kit from the inventory screen without restarting a client, after disconnecting Arena. Otherwise things which you put inside new Kit won't be things you expect and see (actual items will be visible after client restart).
If you create your kits before you go to Arena everything should be fine. Kits can be selected in-game by pressing 'i'
