Welcome to the community Wiki for the C4 Engine. This is a collection of articles about C4 written by both its creators and its users. The type of information on this Wiki ranges from introductory overviews to detailed coding examples.

To edit pages, you must be registered in the forums and be a member of one of the licensed users groups for the Standard Edition or higher. Use the same login name and password to access the Wiki. (If you are a licensed C4 user, you can request membership in a licensed users group on the forums by going to the User Control Panel, selecting the Usergroups tab, and joining the group corresponding to your type of license. Your request will then be approved within 24 hours.)
